TAVI Desk: missing and partly built features

Compared with Zoho Desk Enterprise on 2 Nov 2026. These are the 36 features from the go-live inventory of 131 that TAVI Desk does not have (Missing) or has only in part (Partly), including what Wave 1 found. Each entry says what Zoho has, what TAVI has today, and the Plane item if one exists. P0 is needed for go-live, P1 soon after, P2 later.
