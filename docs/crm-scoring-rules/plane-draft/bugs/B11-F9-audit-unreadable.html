Summary

Scoring rule changes are logged, but an edit to a rule's lines is shown as "EntriesJson: value: 1 item → value: 1 item". The admin cannot see what changed (for example points 15 → 20).

Environment

  • Staging, staging-crm.taviportal.com, tenant NDC-Staging, 12 Nov 2026
  • Owner account

Steps to reproduce

  1. Edit a scoring rule and change one line's points from 15 to 20.
  2. Open Audit Log and find the scoring rule "updated" entry.

Expected result

The entry shows the old and new line (field, condition, value, points), or at least "points 15 → 20".

Actual result

  • Changes: EntriesJson "value: 1 item" → "value: 1 item", RowVersion 4 → 5, UpdatedAt.
  • Created and deleted entries are logged correctly.
