# Zoho CRM Enterprise: feature inventory (reference column for the TAVI CRM go-live matrix)

Compiled 25 Oct 2026 from public Zoho sources only (help.zoho.com CRM KB, zoho.com/crm/developer/docs, the edition comparison page and the complete feature list page). This was read-only web research. Nothing was logged into or changed.

**How to read this**
- One line per feature: `- **Feature** — what / key options / limits — edition — source`.
- **Enterprise** = included in Enterprise (and therefore in Ultimate). **Ultimate-only** = Ultimate and not Enterprise. **Add-on** = paid extra or separate Zoho product, whatever the edition. **Enterprise (limit differs)** = in both, with a higher Ultimate cap that is shown.
- **(inferred)** = the docs read do not state it. It comes from product behaviour or related statements and should be confirmed on the live Zoho org before it is used as a pass/fail oracle.
- **CONFLICT** = two official Zoho pages disagree. Both values are given.
- Edition sources:
  - **CMP** is https://www.zoho.com/crm/comparison.html. The page itself says it was last updated 4 Jun 2022.
  - **CFL** is https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html. It is newer, but its table was scraped without the check-mark images, so the column alignment of some rows (marked "CFL, verify") is uncertain.
- The automation features already researched in depth (assignment rules, review, workflow, approval, blueprint, order of execution) are listed here in one line each. ## 1 Modules & records

- **Leads** — prospect records with standard fields, Lead Status, Lead Source and conversion to Contact, Account and Deal — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/leads/articles/standard-fields-leads
- **Contacts** — people linked to an Account. They hold the email opt-out, the portal-invite source and Contact Roles on Deals — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/contacts
- **Accounts** — companies, with a parent-account hierarchy, and related lists for Contacts, Deals, Cases and inventory records — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/accounts
- **Deals** — opportunities with Stage, Amount, Closing Date and Probability (stage-mapped), plus Contact Roles and Competitors — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/deal-management/articles/standard-fields-deals
- **Multiple pipelines (Deals)** — each pipeline has its own stage set per layout. Limit is 20 on Enterprise and 50 on Ultimate — Enterprise (limit differs) — https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html
- **Team selling and deal split** — several users credited on one deal, with the revenue split by percent — Enterprise (inferred) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/deal-management/articles/team-selling-and-deal-split
- **Tasks** — to-dos with Due Date, Status, Priority, reminder and a recurring option. They can be related to any record (What Id / Who Id) — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/activities/articles/working-with-tasks-and-meetings
- **Meetings (Events)** — calendar events with participants (users, contacts, leads), invitations, recurrence, reminders, online-meeting link and a check-in location — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/activities/articles/working-with-meetings-new
- **Calls** — inbound or outbound call logs, scheduled calls, call result and duration, and telephony-created calls — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/activities/articles/log-calls
- **Recurring activities** — repeat patterns for tasks and meetings (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly) — Enterprise — https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html
- **Products** — catalogue with unit price, tax, stock, vendor, usage unit and product-active flag — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-inventory/products
- **Price Books** — pricing models (flat or differential list price by quantity range) that link products to list prices — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-inventory/price-books
- **Quotes** — line-item subform (Quoted Items) with discount, tax and adjustment, quote stage, inventory template PDF, email and conversion to SO or Invoice — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-inventory/quotes
- **Sales Orders** — line items, conversion to Invoice, and stock deduction (inferred) — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-inventory/sales-orders
- **Purchase Orders** — vendor orders with line items, and stock increase on receipt (inferred) — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-inventory/purchase-orders
- **Invoices** — line items, status, template PDF and email. Can be synced with Zoho Books or Finance — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-inventory/invoices
- **Vendors** — supplier records related to Products and Purchase Orders — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-inventory/vendors
- **Cases** — support tickets with Status, Priority, Origin, related Account, Contact and Product, solutions and escalation — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customer-support/articles/working-with-cases
- **Solutions** — knowledge articles. They can be published to the portal and related to Cases — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customer-support/articles/working-with-solutions
- **Campaigns** — marketing campaigns with type, status, budget, expected and actual revenue, member Leads and Contacts with member status, and parent campaign — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/marketing-automation-tools/campaigns
- **Forecasts** — a tab for targets and achievement by hierarchy. Detailed in area 6 — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/forecasts/articles/creating-and-working-with-forecasts
- **Documents** — document library with folders, folder sharing, file versioning and reviews. It runs on WorkDrive for new sign-ups — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/document-management
- **Visits** — website visit records captured by the Zoho SalesIQ integration (pages viewed, time spent, visitor score) — Enterprise (needs SalesIQ, which is a separate product) (inferred) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/connect-with-customers/visitor-tracking-with-salesiq
- **Projects** — Zoho Projects tab and related list, with projects created from Deals or Accounts — Add-on (Zoho Projects subscription) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/integrations/zoho
- **Services & Appointments** — a Services catalogue (duration, price, members, location) plus an Appointments module with booking, reschedule and cancel, and status — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/services
- **Appointment booking** — appointments booked for services from records or a booking page — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/activities/articles/booking-appointments-for-services
- **Notes** — rich notes on records with attachments, mentions and threaded replies. Notes on a parent roll up to it — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/attachments-and-notes
- **Attachments** — files on a record, from desktop, Docs, WorkDrive, Google Drive and others — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/attachments-and-notes
- **Create record** — full create form, quick create (layout-specific) and the "Save and New" option — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/common-operations-with-records
- **Clone record** — copies a record into a new create form for changes — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/common-operations-with-records
- **Edit and inline edit** — full edit, or inline edit from the detail page and list view (pencil on a cell) — Enterprise (inferred; the page does not cover inline edit) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/navigate-records
- **Delete record** — single or bulk. Deleting a parent also removes its notes, activities and children. Needs the Delete permission — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/common-operations-with-records
- **Recycle Bin (restore)** — deleted records and files are kept for 60 days and can be restored. Only admins can purge permanently. Purge does not fire delete workflows — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/recycle-bin/articles/recycle-bin
- **Record navigation** — previous and next record from the detail page, records per page, and first or last page — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/navigate-records
- **Record detail page** — Overview, Timeline and related-list sections, a business card (up to 5 fields), and a canvas detail view option — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/record-level-customization/articles/page-level-customization
- **Related lists** — related records, open and closed activities, emails, notes, attachments, products, campaigns and invited meetings. They can be reordered or hidden, and have an expanded view — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/record-level-customization/articles/using-related-lists-expanded-view
- **Contextual custom related lists** — related lists filtered or built from a function, per module — Enterprise (not in Professional) — https://www.zoho.com/crm/comparison.html
- **Timeline** — chronological history of field changes, activities, emails, notes, attachments, automation runs and upcoming scheduled actions. Can be filtered — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/timeline/articles/timeline-in-record-detail-page
- **Tags** — colour tags on records, added manually, by workflow, by import or by API. Limit is 10 per record, and 100 per module on Enterprise (200 on Ultimate) — Enterprise (limit differs) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/working-with-tags
- **Follow record** — a user follows a record to get feed updates about its changes — Enterprise (inferred) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/collaborate-and-increase-team-productivity/feeds
- **Emails related list on record** — sent and received emails linked to the record, with thread view and open and click status — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/connect-with-customers/email/user-functions/articles/email-communication-send-and-receive-mails
- **Social on record** — social profile and interactions (Facebook and X) shown on Lead and Contact records — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/connect-with-customers/social/articles/social-profiles
- **Add photo / record image** — image on Leads, Contacts, Accounts, Deals, Products, Cases, Solutions, Vendors and custom modules — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/record-level-customization/articles/add-photo
- **Associate other records** — link existing records (for example campaigns or products) from the detail page — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/associate-other-records
- **Print record / print view / mailing labels** — print preview with an email, mail-merge or inventory template, list print view and mailing labels — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/printing-mailing-labels
- **Lead conversion** — creates a Contact, creates an Account if Company is filled, and optionally a Deal. Owner choice. Matching to an existing account or contact by unique fields, then email, company or name. One-way. Needs the Convert Leads permission — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/leads/articles/convert-leads
- **Lead conversion mapping** — maps custom Lead fields to Contact, Account and Deal fields (same type and length). Layout-based mapping, and subform mapping one row at a time — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/leads/articles/convert-leads
- **Mass convert leads** — bulk conversion. Skips validation rules and subform mapping, uses the default pipeline, and shows only mandatory Deal fields — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/leads/articles/convert-leads
- **Conversion of related data** — notes move to the Deal (copied to the Account and Contact), attachments go to a target the user chooses, and tags carry over — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/sales-force-automation/leads/articles/convert-leads
- **Find and merge duplicates** — manual duplicate search on up to 6 fields, merging with a choice of master and field values — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/duplication-management/articles/merge-duplicate-record
- **De-duplicate (auto-merge)** — scans a module on chosen de-dupe fields and merges identical records automatically. Auto-merge does not fire edit or delete workflows — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/duplication-management/articles/auto-merge-duplicates
- **Duplicate check / unique fields** — "Do not allow duplicate values", case-insensitive, on Text, Email, Phone, Integer, Long Integer and URL. Not on Tasks, Calls or Meetings. Limit is 2 per module (CFL) — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/duplication-management/articles/check-duplicate-records
- **Mass update** — bulk field update. Text Area and Lookup are not allowed. Multi-select picklists can overwrite or append. Manual selection is capped at 500 records, or "select all in view" for more. Needs a permission — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/common-operations-with-records
- **Mass delete** — delete records by criteria search. Needs the Mass Delete permission. Capped at 500 per manual selection — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/common-operations-with-records
- **Mass transfer** — move records from one owner to another by search criteria. Needs the Mass Transfer permission — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/common-operations-with-records
- **Change owner** — single or bulk, with an option to transfer open activities, tasks and deals. Needs the Change Owner permission — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/common-operations-with-records
- **Mass email / mass actions from list** — send emails, add tags, enrol in a cadence, run a macro and others from the list selection — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/connect-with-customers/email/user-functions/articles/sending-mass-emails
- **Record sharing (manual)** — share an individual record with users, roles or groups as Read Only or Read/Write, optionally with related lists — Enterprise (not in Professional) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/security-control/manage-data-sharing/articles/share-records
- **Record locking** — rule-based or manual lock of records by criteria. Locked fields and actions are restricted and blueprint transitions are hidden. Limit is 5 rules per module (CFL). Detail in ZDI — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/record-level-customization/articles/record-locking-configuration-and-usage
- **Approve records (awaiting)** — web-form and import records held for manual approval in "Approve Leads/Contacts/Cases". Detail in ZDI — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/approve-records
- **List view** — columns, sort, record count, records per page (up to 100 per page per CFL), pinned columns (inferred) and quick filters — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/managing-module-views/articles/list-view
- **Custom views** — saved criteria views with chosen columns and sort order, shared with users, roles or groups, or kept private. Unlimited in number. A favourite or default view can be set — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/managing-module-views/articles/module-views
- **Kanban view** — cards grouped by a picklist (for example Stage), drag and drop to update, and aggregates per column. Limit is 5 kanban views on Enterprise (10 on Ultimate, CFL) — Enterprise (limit differs) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/managing-module-views/articles/kanban-views
- **Canvas list view** — designed list layout with images, buttons and styled field labels. Limit is 3 views per user per module on Enterprise (CMP) — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/canvas/articles/customizing-module-view-using-the-all-new-canvas-design-suite
- **Sheet view (Zoho Sheet)** — spreadsheet-style bulk edit of a view's records, synced back to the CRM — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/managing-module-views/articles/using-zoho-sheet-view
- **Map view** — records plotted on a map by their address field — Enterprise (inferred) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/managing-module-views/articles/map-view
- **Chart view** — a module's records shown as a chart in the view switcher. Limit is 5 (CFL) — Enterprise — https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html
- **Sorting** — sort a list by a column, ascending or descending. A default sort can be saved per custom view — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/managing-module-views/articles/list-view
- **Advanced filters** — ad-hoc filter panel on the list (system filters, filter by fields, related activity and email status). Results can be acted on with macros and follow-ups — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/advanced-filters
- **Smart filters** — saved quick filters on a list. Limit is 10 (CFL) — Enterprise — https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html
- **Alphabet search** — A–Z letter filter in a module, on First Name by default (changeable through Name Format) — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/search-records
- **Global search** — `/` shortcut, a module scope dropdown, and `#` to search Record Name or Tags only. Minimum 3 characters. Only records the user may see are returned. Covers all standard, inventory, finance, service and custom modules, and up to 100 linking modules — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/search-records
- **Advanced search** — filter, sort and pick modules in the search results, with up to 10 result columns. Notes are searchable here only — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/search-records
- **Zia search (Ask Zia in search)** — `/` command in global search to find reports, dashboards and more. Needs English as the personal language and Enterprise with 20 or more licences, or Ultimate — Enterprise (20+ users) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/search-records
- **Search across Zoho** — extends global search to other Zoho apps and help pages — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/manage-crm-data/record-management/articles/search-records
- **Import wizard** — accepts CSV, XLS, XLSX and VCF (VCF only for Leads, Contacts and Users). Max 25 MB and 500 columns per file. Covers modules, plus parent records with up to 100 related records each — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/import-data/articles/import-data-into-a-crm-module
- **Import row limit** — **CONFLICT**: the KB gives 100,000 rows per file on Enterprise (200,000 on Ultimate), CMP gives 30,000 per batch, and CFL gives 50,000. The Users module allows 1,000 — Enterprise (limit differs) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/import-data/articles/import-data-into-a-crm-module
- **Import field mapping** — auto-map, default values, create new fields, format choice for dates and numbers, replace empty values, and lookup match by ID, unique field, name, email or external ID — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/import-data/articles/import-data-into-a-crm-module
- **Import duplicate handling** — Add as new (skip duplicates on a chosen unique field), Update existing only, or Both. "Skip empty values" option — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/import-data/articles/import-data-into-a-crm-module
- **Import options: owner, approval, automation, tags** — assign owner by an assignment rule, require manual approval, a "Trigger automation and process management" checkbox, a follow-up task, and tags to replace or append — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/import-data/articles/import-data-into-a-crm-module
- **Undo import / import history** — added records can be undone within 30 days (updates cannot). The detailed summary and error file are kept 7 days and the summary 30 days. CFL says history is kept 60 days — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/import-data/articles/view-import-history
- **Data migration wizard** — migrate from another CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot and others) or from a CSV bundle, with module mapping — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/data-migration
- **Export** — export a module to CSV (all or a view) and export reports. Needs the Export permission. CFL limit is 200,000 records per module and 500 exports per day — Enterprise (limit differs) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/export-data/articles/export-crm-data
- **Data backup** — scheduled (twice a month or monthly) or on-demand. 2 free backups a month on Enterprise and 4 on Ultimate (CFL says 4 for both, CONFLICT). CSV in ZIP, with a separate attachments ZIP. Link valid 7 days. Excludes emails, documents and integration data — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/data-backup/articles/requesting-data-backup

- **Sticky notes** — personal notes on screen — Enterprise — https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html
- **Business card view** — up to 5 key fields at the top of the detail page — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/record-level-customization/articles/business-card-view

## 2 Customization

- **Customize standard modules** — rename, reorder and hide tabs, edit layouts, and set module permissions per profile — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-modules/articles/customize-modules
- **Custom modules** — org or team modules with singular and plural names, a record-name field, profile permissions and sections. **CONFLICT** on the limit: the KB gives 200 on Enterprise (500 on Ultimate), custom and team combined, CFL also gives 200, and CMP gives 100 (2 per user licence). Deleting a module is permanent — Enterprise (limit differs) — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-modules/articles/customize-modules
- **Team modules / teamspaces** — modules owned by a team rather than the org, with their own users and profiles — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-modules/articles/build-new-modules-to-bring-every-unique-customer-facing-process-inside-zoho-crm
- **Linking modules (many-to-many)** — a junction module that links two modules, for example Contacts and Products — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-modules/articles/linking-modules
- **Tab groups** — group tabs in the navigation. Limit is 25 on Enterprise (50 on Ultimate, CFL) — Enterprise (limit differs) — https://www.zoho.com/crm/comparison.html
- **Custom fields: count** — **CONFLICT**: CMP gives 300 per module on Enterprise and 500 on Ultimate. CFL gives 500 per module (230 for activities). Unused fields still count — Enterprise (limit differs) — https://www.zoho.com/crm/comparison.html
- **Field type: single line** — text up to 255 characters (inferred). Can be unique, external ID, mandatory or encrypted — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-fields/articles/types-of-fields
- **Field type: multi-line (small, large, rich text)** — small holds 2,000 characters (filterable, encryptable). Large holds 32,000 (not filterable). Rich text holds 50,000 including HTML, is not in lists or filters, and is limited to 2 in Tasks and Meetings and 5 elsewhere — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-fields/articles/types-of-fields
- **Field type: email, phone, URL** — format-validated text types. Can be unique. Phone gives click-to-call — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-fields/articles/types-of-fields
- **Field type: picklist** — values can be added, renamed, deleted (a deleted value stays on records as unused), sorted (entered or alphabetical), given a default and made mandatory. Limit is 15 picklists per module (CFL) — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-fields/articles/types-of-fields
- **Field type: multi-select picklist** — many values per record. Import uses `;` as the separator — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-fields/articles/types-of-fields
- **Field type: radio button** — a visible single choice of at most 3 options per layout. Not in Users — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-fields/articles/types-of-fields
- **Field type: number, long integer, decimal, percent, currency** — min and max limits and an optional slider (up to 9 intervals, not in subforms). Currency has decimals and rounding. Long integer is capped at 35 per module (CFL) — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-fields/articles/types-of-fields
- **Field type: date, date/time** — 50 of each per module (CFL) — Enterprise — https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html
- **Field type: checkbox** — 80 per module (CFL) — Enterprise — https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html
- **Field type: auto number** — prefix, start, suffix and date pattern (in the company time zone). Numbers can be skipped. 1 per module — Enterprise — https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-fields/articles/types-of-fields
