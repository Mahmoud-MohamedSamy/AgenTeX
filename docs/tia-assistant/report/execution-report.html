Verdict

Tia is safe and fast. It is not yet reliable for everyday questions. It never showed data a user could not see, the setup page and action cards work, and answers start in under 2 seconds. But simple questions often fail with a generic or customer-bot reply, internal tool errors reach the user, and several things the product offers (private ticket comments, most setup proposals, Agent Instruction) do not work.

Headlines

  • Works: access and data scope (every K case passes: hidden leads, other departments, no CRM app, other tenant header, guessed ids); setup lifecycle (save, deploy, deactivate, delete, Recycle Bin restore, conflict checks); on/off switch, apps, audience, topics, credit caps; action cards (confirm, cancel, expiry, double click, records logged with channel "tia"); panel UI, Arabic right to left, script-safe rendering.
  • Speed: median 1.6 s to the first text and 2.1 s to the full answer; counts and lists 4–6 s; no timeouts.
  • Main problems: 9 bugs drafted (below), 3 of them High.
  • Blocked: CRM browser checks. The CRM admin's sign-in loops at the organisation picker and the owner's saved CRM session had expired; CRM was covered through the API.
  • Cleanup: all test data and chats deleted; Tia, the AI policy, runtime settings and the Desk Agent profile equal the starting baseline.
