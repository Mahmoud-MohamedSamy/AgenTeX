Verdict

Tia keeps data safe and is fast, and its setup page, action cards and panel work in CRM, Desk, Omnichannel and Analytics. It is not ready for everyday use: simple questions still fail or give wrong counts, internal errors still reach users, and only 1 of 134 setup areas can really be proposed. The filed bugs changed shape between 23 and 24 Nov rather than going away.

Headlines

  • Safe: every access and data-scope case passes, including a field hidden by field permission (K-03), a suspended user (A-22, session revoked at once) and other departments and apps.
  • Works: setup lifecycle, conflicts, the on/off switch, apps, audience, topics, credit caps and the daily-limit message; action cards; workflow rules run on records Tia creates (J-18); the panel in every app, Arabic, reload recovery.
