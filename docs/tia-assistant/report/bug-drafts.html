Tia | Simple questions fail with "Sorry, I couldn't answer that just now" or "would you prefer to speak with one of our team?"

Steps to reproduce

  1. Sign in to Desk as a Desk Agent who has "Chat with Tia".
  2. Open the Ask Tia panel and start a new chat.
  3. Click the suggested question "How many open tickets do I have?".
  4. In a new chat, ask "Count the tickets with status Open that I can see".

Account

Desk Agent (Customer Experience Specialists 2, with "Chat with Tia"); Desk Administrator (QA Team 1); owner

Expected result

Both questions give the number of open tickets the agent can see (40 at the time of the test).

Actual result

The suggested question fails every time (3 of 3, at 14:37, about 15:20 and about 15:40 UTC) with: "I can't confirm that from our systems right now, and I don't want to guess. Would you like me to try again, or would you prefer to speak with one of our team?". The reworded question returns 40, which is correct. The second sentence is wording for customers and does not belong in a staff assistant.

  • Same result in Arabic: "كم عدد التذاكر المفتوحة لدي؟" gets the Arabic version of the same reply (I-28).
  • "How many chats are waiting right now?" (Desk admin), twice: the customer-bot reply (I-22).
  • "Show my QA TIA deal" (CRM), twice: "Sorry, I couldn't answer that just now. Please try rephrasing your question." 3 such deals exist (I-24).
  • Leads, contacts and deals counted in one question, twice: the same "Sorry…" reply (I-09).
  • "What did the customer say in ticket QA TIA T3 last week?", twice: the same "Sorry…" reply; expected "there is no such message" (I-30).
  • Run a saved report by name: "invalid reporting definition", or the customer-bot reply (I-10).
  • Ask about a blueprint: "Sorry…", or "I need the specific module name or key…" on a Desk ticket blueprint (I-18).
