Rebuild of NDC's setup on TAVI and follow-up tickets (3–4 Nov 2026)

On 3 Nov we rebuilt NDC's core Zoho Desk setup on NDC-Staging, prefixed "QA NDC" and kept as the NDC reference setup: 8 core departments (Manual Invoice, NDC Finance, Financial approval, Accounting, Quality Assurance, Client Complaints, Clients' Financial Investigations, FLY-WT Refund), the 23 branch departments their workflows share into, 32 teams, 41 statuses, 94 custom fields, 10 layouts, 7 layout rules, 1 validation rule, 6 blueprints (81 transitions), 42 of 80 core workflows (the rest were blocked by the old 50-rule limit), 9 SLA policies and the notification settings. Then we ran smoke tickets.

Works like NDC: custom statuses with types, large pick lists (up to 188 values), decimal, currency and date fields, layout rules (show fields or sections, require fields), the "Reject needs a reason" validation rule, blueprints with team and ticket-owner transitions, the share-ticket workflow, SLA policies with NDC's targets and escalations, per-department notification rules, and clearing the owner from a workflow by assigning to a team.

Gaps filed as separate tickets (retested 4 Nov)

  • NDC-2793 Trigger "on create, and later when these fields change" — Done.
  • NDC-2795 Same rule name in different departments — Done.
  • NDC-2797 Ticket statuses per department and layout — Done. The status change when a ticket moves onto a layout (fall-back to default) is still to compare with NDC's Zoho.
  • NDC-2798 Team notification when a comment is edited or deleted — Done.
  • NDC-2791 "Mark as Required" on one layout makes the field required on every layout, and now blocks saving tickets in every department — Bug.
  • NDC-2794 Team with agent in one rule: the agent is set but the team is cleared — Bug.
  • NDC-2796 Several layouts per department: works, but the create form does not pre-select the department's default layout and offers every layout — Bug.
