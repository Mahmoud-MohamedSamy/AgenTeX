How NDC works

The gaps come from one process: tickets arrive by phone and chat, are routed per branch, shared across departments and approved through blueprint chains.

  1. Intake. Mostly phone and chat, then email, WhatsApp/Telegram ("NDC Portal", "FlyWT customer support"), the help center and two feedback widgets.
  2. Routing. 30 direct-assignment rules send new tickets to a team or agent by Status, Created By, Layout or Staff Action. 24 round-robin rules load-balance to agents or teams; overdue, escalated, response-overdue and "Bad"-rated tickets have their own targets.
  3. Sharing across departments. 75 of 92 workflows run "Share Ticket": Quality Assurance, Financial approval, Clients' Financial Investigations and Client Complaints share each ticket to the branch in the field Related Branch, unless Feedback = Pass. Other actions: Assign Ticket (20), Send Email Reply (10), field updates (7), skill-based assignment (1).
  4. Approval chains as blueprints on Status. ADM (8 states, 18 transitions), 2nd check (7 states), Financial approvals (11 states), Clients' Financial Investigations (11 states, 24 transitions), flywt-refund (7 states). Transitions owned by the ticket owner (74) or a team (11); 63 field updates and 32 alerts after transitions; 10 transitions ask for mandatory fields.
  5. Quality audit. QA tickets carry a scoring sheet in custom fields; status ends as Pass Audit / Rejected Audit / Review Audit Manager (965 K are Pass Audit).
  6. Finance. E-Top-Up approval statuses, refund, ADM, GL and bank-deposit fields. A validation rule blocks Status = Reject without a rejection reason.
  7. Notifications. Per-department notification rules with about 65 email templates each.
  8. AI. Zia summary, sentiment, writing assistant, reply assistance and a Zia workflow action are active; field prediction suggests owner and priority; a ChatGPT extension is installed.

TAVI defects that hit NDC (found 2 Nov, not filed)

  • An approval can name an approver outside the ticket's department.
  • History shows "Followers was updated" as a raw user-id array.
  • An existing reply draft pre-fills the comment editor.
  • An Open ticket can be archived and then disappears from All Tickets.
  • AI calls return 404 (M-04).

Not used by NDC: low priority

Macros, supervisor rules, schedules, custom functions, webhooks, custom buttons, products, contracts, holiday lists, Answer Bot, guided conversations, Telegram (switched off), telephony (phone tickets are typed in). Tags exist but none is on a ticket; ticket approvals and time entries show no use. Facebook / X / Instagram brands exist but have no tickets.

Open questions for NDC

  • Phone (mostly answered): agents log phone tickets by hand. Confirm no call recording or CTI is expected in TAVI.
  • Chat: which websites embed the chat widget, and must chat bots or offline forms move too?
  • WhatsApp and IM Talk: which WhatsApp Business provider sends "NDC Portal outbound", and does the FlyWT web widget stay?
  • Reports: do managers also use Zoho Analytics beyond the Overview, Request, SLA and TOPUP dashboards?
  • Extensions: which of the 9 are still used (Agent Attendance Tracker and Related Tickets by Custom Fields look critical)?
  • Migration: how much of the 2.5 M tickets, 886 agents and custom-module data must move?

How we accessed NDC's Zoho

  • Account: ahegazy@ndceg.com (Support Administrator), signed in twice on 2 Nov 2026, about 12:17 and 14:07, each with a OneAuth push approved by our team. Both show in the account's login history; the second session may still be active and count toward the account's limit of 2 sessions. Before the second sign-in one of the account's sessions had to be ended; we did not do that.
  • Read-only guard: the browser blocked every write call to Zoho (297 calls, all Zoho's own telemetry and presence pings) and, in the second pass, every GET that looked like an action (20 calls). No ticket was opened, so nothing was marked as read.
  • One exception (first pass): viewing Setup → Integrations → Others let Zoho's own page send CtiApiConfig.do?mode=generatePhoneBridgeOauthtoken (a GET). By its name it may have generated a PhoneBridge token; no visible setting changed. The second-pass guard blocked it every time.
  • Allowed on purpose: 20 small POST calls to Zoho's notification-badge and interface-text endpoints.
  • Sign-in prompts: the session-limit warning was dismissed with "Do it later" and the unverified-domain notice with its remind-later option; no domain or session setting changed.
  • Data kept: the raw capture (setup, rules, staff names and emails, metadata of 150 recent tickets) stays on the tester's machine and is excluded from git.
