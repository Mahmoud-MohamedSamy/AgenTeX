NDC Zoho Desk usage: features TAVI Desk is missing

State BacklogPriority HighAssignees MahmoudMohamed, mfathy, Hegazy, eahmedRelates to NDC-2760Updated 3 Nov 2026

NDC runs about 2.5 million tickets in Zoho Desk. We read NDC's Zoho read-only, then rebuilt NDC's core setup on TAVI (NDC-Staging) to test it. TAVI misses 6 features NDC needs to go live (live chat, WhatsApp/IM, AI, agent scale, dashboards, blueprint fixes) plus a hard limit of 50 workflow rules, and 8 things need adjusting, including per-department ticket statuses.

How NDC works

  1. Agents type in most requests as Phone tickets; customers also use live chat, the FlyWT widget, WhatsApp, email and the help center.
  2. Each department has its own form (layout) and its own status list.
  3. Direct-assignment and round-robin rules route tickets to teams and agents.
  4. Workflows share tickets into the branch named in "Related Branch", move finance tickets to teams, close or reopen tickets, send complaint letters.
  5. Refunds, ADM disputes and invoices go through blueprint approval chains on Status.
  6. SLAs set due times; QA audits tickets; managers watch the Overview, Request, SLA and TOPUP dashboards; Zia AI helps agents.
