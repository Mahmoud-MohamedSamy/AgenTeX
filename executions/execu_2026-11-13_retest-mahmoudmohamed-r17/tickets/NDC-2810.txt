# NDC-2810 CRM ⇄ Desk sync fails on contacts and accounts that no longer exist in either app
Summary
When an admin syncs customers between CRM and Desk, the sync doesn't create or update anything. It reports failures for contacts and accounts that don't exist in CRM or Desk anymore, and the Failure Log fills up with them. The same records fail again on every run, so the sync never finishes cleanly.
Steps to Reproduce
- In Desk, open Customers → Contacts (1 contact) and Accounts (no accounts).
- In CRM, open Customers (no records).
- In CRM, go to Settings → CRM ⇄ Desk Integration → Sync History.
- Click Sync all customers and wait for it to finish.
- Open the Failure Log tab.
Expected Result
- The sync only looks at contacts and accounts that exist in CRM or Desk right now.
- Records deleted in either app are unlinked and left out, not reported as failures.
- With nothing new to sync, the run finishes with no failures.
Actual Result
- The sync picks up 11 records, creates or updates none, and marks 9 as "Needs attention".
- The Failure Log lists 372 records, such as "B04", "test test", "zxc asd", "1" and "2". None of them appear in CRM's or Desk's customer lists.
- Most say "The linked Desk record is in the recycle bin". One says Last Name is missing.
- Every run gives the same result: the earlier runs today all ended with 9 records needing attention.
- Sync History shows hundreds of failed contacts and accounts per day.
Environment
- App: TAVI CRM (staging-crm.taviportal.com/settings/integrations/desk) and TAVI Desk (staging-desk.taviportal.com)
- Browser: Edge 156.0.8078.80
- OS: Windows 10
