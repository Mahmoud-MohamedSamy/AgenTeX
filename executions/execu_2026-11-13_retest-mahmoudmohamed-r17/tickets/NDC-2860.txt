# NDC-2860 Record Form | An open dropdown detaches from its field when the form is scrolled and covers the Save button
On the record form, opening any dropdown and then scrolling leaves the list stranded at the top of the window. It no longer sits under the field it belongs to, and it covers the app header — including the Save button — so the form cannot be saved until the list is dismissed.
Steps to Reproduce
- Open Tickets and click + to create a ticket.
- Click the Department field so its list opens.
- Scroll the form down while the list is still open.
- Look at where the list is, and at where the Department field is.
- Try to click Save without closing the list.
- Repeat with the Ticket Owner field — the same thing happens.
Expected Result
- The list stays attached to its field as the page scrolls, or closes once the field leaves the view.
- The list never covers the app header, the global search box, or the Save button.
- It is always obvious which field the list belongs to.
Actual Result
- The list stays open and sits at the top of the window, no longer under its field.
- With Department, the field itself scrolls out of view while its list is still showing.
- The list is drawn over the app header, hiding the global search box and the Save button.
- Save cannot be clicked while a list is open on a scrolled form.
- The list ends up floating next to unrelated fields, so it looks like it belongs to whichever field it has landed near.
Environment
TAVI Desk — staging, Create Ticket form (staging-desk.taviportal.com/modules/88e8ea44…/records/new) · Edge 156.0.8078.80 · Windows 10 · 1272×866 · reporter noles40331 · captured 11 Nov 2026
Notes
Seen on two different fields (Department at 0:19 and Ticket Owner at 0:10 in the recording), so it is the dropdown control rather than one field. Worth confirming when fixing: does the panel glide with the page and stop at the header, or does it stay frozen at the coordinate where it opened? The two point at different fixes.
