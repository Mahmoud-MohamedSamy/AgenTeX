# NDC-2635 Activity Life Cycle: features Zoho Desk has that Desk is missing
Summary
We compared Desk's Activity Life Cycle (tasks, calls and events) with Zoho Desk Activities. This ticket lists what Zoho has and Desk does not have yet. There is no single PRD for activities; this is item "Activity Life Cycle" of NDC-1865. Earlier activity gaps are in NDC-1782 (only SMS reminders left) and NDC-2008 (Phase 3), and are not repeated here.
Already done in Desk
- Tasks, calls and events can be added from the Activities page, from a ticket's ACTIVITY tab (ticket pre-filled) and from contacts and accounts.
- Task statuses match Zoho. Mark as Complete fills Completed Time; Reopen Task goes back to Not Started.
- Calls: Start Call, End Call with outcome (notes go to the ticket as a private comment), Mark as missed, Cancel Call, Reopen Call, Reschedule. A completed call is locked on its page.
- Events: Mark as Completed, Cancel, Reopen Event. An event does not close by itself when its time passes, same as Zoho.
- Overdue badge on open activities whose time has passed.
- 18 task views (Open, Overdue, Today's, Tomorrow's, Today + Overdue, Next 7 Days + Overdue, My …, Spam and more) showing the right records, plus custom views.
- Classic, Compact and Table layouts, sort, search, filter, Export and bulk Update.
- Reminders: up to 5 per activity, pop-up and email, with clear messages for every wrong value. The pop-up arrived 5 seconds after the due time.
- Comments (with @mention, edit, delete), attachments, time entry and timer on activities.
- Desk also has a Calendar, event invitees with RSVP and a timer, which Zoho Desk does not have.
Missing — settings
- Department on every activity. In Zoho it is required, it limits the owner list to that department, and an activity is only seen inside its department. Desk forms have no Department, and an admin outside Customer Support can open and edit the activities of Customer Support tickets that are 404 for them.
Missing — list and page actions
- Delete on the task, call or event page (Zoho: Delete, goes to the Recycle Bin). Desk can only delete through the API.
- Delete in the bulk bar (Zoho mass actions: Update, Delete, Assign To). Desk's bulk bar has Update only.
Missing — where activities show
- Activity count on the ticket (Zoho returns taskCount). Desk ticket counts are replies, comments, attachments and time entries only.
Missing — API
- A ticket-activities endpoint and ready-made filters (Zoho: GET /tickets/{id}/activities, dueDate=Overdue / Today / Tomorrow, /tasks/count). Desk only has the generic records API; /desk/activities, /desk/activities/stats and /desk/activities/reports return 404.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com, Edge, 26 October 2026, Desk Agent and Desk Admin accounts.
