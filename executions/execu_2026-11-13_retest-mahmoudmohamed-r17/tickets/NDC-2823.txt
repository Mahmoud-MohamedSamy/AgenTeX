# NDC-2823 Tickets | No option to select all tickets at once in the ticket list view. (Zoho Desk offers a "Select All" master checkbox)
TAVI Desk's ticket list has no master checkbox and no "Select All" option. Once one or more tickets are ticked, the selection chip offers only Clear selection; there is no control in the list header, in the selection chip, or under More that selects every ticket in the current view. Tickets must be ticked one row at a time.
On the All Tickets view (377 records on staging), selecting the whole view would take 377 individual clicks, which makes the bulk actions — Assign To, Update, Close, Merge — unusable at list scale.
Zoho Desk places a master checkbox at the far left of the bulk-action bar; hovering it reads "Select All" and one click selects every ticket in the view.
Steps to reproduce
- Sign in to staging-desk.taviportal.com
- Open Tickets → Views → All Tickets (377 records)
- Tick any single ticket to reveal the bulk-action bar
- Look for a select-all control in the list header, in the selection chip, and under More
Actual result
No master checkbox and no "Select All" entry anywhere. The selection chip shows only the selected count and Clear selection.
Expected result
A master checkbox in the ticket list header selects every ticket shown in the current view in one action, with the selected count displayed and all bulk actions (Assign To, Update, Reply, Comment, Close, Merge, More) applying to the whole selection — matching Zoho Desk.
Environment
TAVI Desk — staging (staging-desk.taviportal.com/views) · Chrome · Tenant Owner profile
