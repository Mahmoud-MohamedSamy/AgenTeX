# NDC-2916 Global Sets | The "+ New Global Set" button text is wrapped onto two lines
On Settings → Modules and Fields → Global Sets, the button that creates a global set renders its label across two lines — the “+” alone on the first, “New Global Set” on the second. Every other primary button in the product puts the icon and label inline on one row.
Steps to Reproduce
- Sign in to staging-crm.taviportal.com.
- Go to Settings → Customization → Modules and Fields.
- Open the Global Sets tab.
- Look at the button at the top right of the page.
Expected Result
- The button reads + New Global Set on a single line, with the icon inline before the label.
- The button widens as needed to fit its content on one row.
- It matches the other primary buttons in CRM and Desk.
Actual Result
- The “+” renders on its own line, centred above the first word.
- “New Global Set” renders beneath it, so the button’s content is stacked into two rows.
- The button is the only one on the page drawn this way.
Environment
TAVI CRM — staging, Settings → Modules and Fields → Global Sets (staging-crm.taviportal.com/settings/modules-and-fields) · Edge · Windows 10 · 1280×984 · Tenant Owner
