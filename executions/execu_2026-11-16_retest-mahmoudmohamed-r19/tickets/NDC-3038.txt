# NDC-3038 Blueprint designer | On a large blueprint the transition labels and buttons overlap; Firefox takes 43 s to open it
Summary
With 30 states and 80 transitions the designer is slow to open (very slow in Firefox) and the transition labels and their buttons cover each other.
Steps to Reproduce
- Create a blueprint with 30 states and 80 transitions.
- Open it in the designer in Edge, Chrome and Firefox.
Expected Result
- Labels readable and clickable on a dense graph.
- The designer opens in a few seconds.
Actual Result
- Labels and their + / delete buttons overlap on every row.
- Time to open: Edge 9.0 s, Chrome 10.7 s, Firefox 43 s. (API create 0.8 s and publish 0.9 s are fine.)
Note
Spec row N8. Not compared with Zoho.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, owner account, 12–13 Nov 2026.
Attachment
Annotated evidence attached (BP-N8-large-blueprint-labels-overlap.png).
