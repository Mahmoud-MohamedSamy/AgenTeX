# NDC-2864 Notifications | Agent notification rules with Email on send no email, only the in-app notification, unless a custom template is chosen
Summary
Agent notification rules such as "Assigning a ticket" show Email switched on and "Sent as: in-app notification + email", but with the default template they send only the in-app notification. No email reaches the agent. The default template is called "the in-app notification only", and a hint says to choose a template to also send an email. In Zoho Desk, an enabled agent notification rule sends the email with its predefined template; customising the template is optional. Agents who are not looking at Desk never hear about tickets assigned to them, to their team, or comments on their tickets.
Steps to Reproduce
- Go to Setup › Customization › Notifications and choose a department.
- Under Agent Notifications, look at "Assigning a ticket": Email is on and the template is "Default — the in-app notification only".
- As the agent, open My Notifications and check that the Email channel and "Tickets assigned to you" email are on.
- Assign a ticket in that department to the agent.
- Check the agent's bell and the agent's mailbox (including Spam).
Expected Result
- As in Zoho Desk: the agent gets an email for the assignment, using the rule's predefined template, because the rule's Email switch is on.
- The same for the other agent rules with Email on (comment added, ticket assigned to the agent's team).
- If TAVI keeps an "in-app only" default, the row must not say Email on and "Sent as: in-app notification + email".
Actual Result
- The bell shows "Ticket #2859 assigned to you" and "Ticket #2818 assigned to you".
- Neither email arrived in the agent’s mailbox (Mahmoud Samy, checked by the user, 11 Nov 2026).
- A newly invited agent got no email either for "Ticket #2856 assigned to you" or for a comment on #2856.
- The rule row shows Email on and "Sent as: in-app notification + email", and the template is "Default — the in-app notification only".
- Retest the same day: after choosing a template on these rules, the emails did arrive (#2865 assigned and a comment on it, team ticket #2869, #2867 assigned). So the emails fail only with the default template.
- The "Good rating on ticket #2818" email did arrive without a template, probably sent by the Customer Happiness setting rather than by the notification rule.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10. Department QA AO Dept A; agents Mahmoud Samy (Desk Administrator) and a new test agent; 11 Nov 2026, 09:48–11:33 UTC. Customer emails from the same department (ticket closed, rating request) do arrive, so outgoing mail works. Zoho source: help.zoho.com "Managing Notification Rules/Triggers in Zoho Desk": "notifications can be sent via e-mail" and "The notification rules have their predefined template" (help page, not checked in a live Zoho Desk).

--- comment 2026-11-16T11:56:38.104481+02:00 by 9529016b-e67d-42b9-815c-263f673b8252
Fix is in review: desk PR #258 and notify PR #24 (notify ships first). Not deployed yet.
Cause, checked on staging 16 Nov: with the default template, Desk handed the agent email to the notification service. NDC-Staging's delivery policy has email off by default, so the email was dropped. In the last 7 days, 809 "ticket assigned" notifications went out in-app only. With a template chosen, Desk sends the email itself, which is why QA's retest worked.
Change (product decision): every agent, team and department rule now sends its built-in email whenever the rule's Email switch is on. Desk sends it itself, so the delivery policy no longer blocks it. A chosen template still replaces the built-in wording. Agents who switched that email off under My Notifications don't get it, and the in-app notification is unchanged. The default template now reads "Default — the built-in email".
Not covered: the built-in agent emails are English only.
To retest after deploy: repeat the original steps with the default template. The agent should get both the bell and the email.
