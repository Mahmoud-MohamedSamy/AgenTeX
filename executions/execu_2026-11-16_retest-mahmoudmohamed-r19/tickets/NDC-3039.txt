# NDC-3039 Blueprint designer | Issue list before publishing: the "name" line can't be clicked and a missing department isn't counted
Summary
The "issues to fix before publishing" box links transition issues, but the blueprint name issue is plain text and an empty Department is not listed.
Steps to Reproduce
- Click New blueprint, add two transitions without names, leave Name and Department empty.
- Look at the issue box above the canvas.
Expected Result
- Every issue is a link that jumps to the item (Name too).
- The empty Department is counted and listed ("4 issues").
Actual Result
- "3 issues to fix before publishing": "The blueprint needs a name" is plain text.
- Department is red on the field but not in the count or list.
Note
Spec row H3 (the rest of H3 passes). Not compared with Zoho.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, owner account, 12–13 Nov 2026.
