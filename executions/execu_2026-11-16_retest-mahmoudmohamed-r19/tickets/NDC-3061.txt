# NDC-3061 Blueprint | The "Send notification" after-transition action is never sent, while the transition's other after-actions run
Summary
A blueprint transition with a Send notification after-action does not notify anyone. The transition runs, and the other after-actions on the same transition work (field updates, create task), but none of the chosen users gets the notification.
Steps to Reproduce
- In Desk → Settings → Blueprints, open a blueprint for a department (Tech, "test blueprint").
- On transition Move to On Hold (Open → On Hold), add the after-action Send notification with title and body "Ticket Moved to On Hold" and four users as recipients. Also add a Create task after-action for comparison. Publish.
- Create a ticket in Tech that enters the blueprint (#4133) and run Move to On Hold.
- Sign in as each recipient and open the notification bell.
Expected Result
- Every chosen user gets "Ticket Moved to On Hold" in the bell right after the transition.
Actual Result
- The transition runs (Open → On Hold).
- Other after-actions on the same transition work: the ticket's Email field is updated, the contact's Description is updated, and the task is created (its "Task assigned to you" notification arrives at 14:41:33).
- None of the four recipients (ndc-staging-owner, mahmoud.mahamed1515, Mahmoud Mohamed, Mahmoud Samy) gets "Ticket Moved to On Hold".
Note
- Desk email (the other after-action) was not checked; the recording does not show the mailbox.
- Zoho Desk not checked live.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), blueprint "test blueprint" (Tech), ticket #4133, transition run at 2026-11-15 14:41:21 UTC, Microsoft Edge 156, Windows 10.
Attachments
