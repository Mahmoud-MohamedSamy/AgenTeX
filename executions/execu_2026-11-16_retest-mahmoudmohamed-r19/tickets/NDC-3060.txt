# NDC-3060 Blueprint | The Contact field in a transition form shows the contact's ID instead of the contact's name
Summary
When a blueprint transition asks for the ticket's Contact field, the form shows the contact's internal ID (de7195a2-369e-48c5-b03f-b8e37d6c5fb2) in a plain text box, instead of the contact's name. The ticket itself shows the contact normally in Contact Info.
Steps to Reproduce
- Create a blueprint for a department (Tech in the screenshot) with a transition, for example Open → On Hold "Move to On Hold". In the transition's During section, add the Contact field.
- Open a ticket in that blueprint that already has a contact (#4058).
- Click Move to On Hold.
- Look at the Contact field in the transition form.
Expected Result
- The Contact field shows the contact's name, the same as Contact Info on the ticket.
- It works as a contact picker, where you search by name and choose, like on the ticket form.
Actual Result
- The Contact field shows de7195a2-369e-48c5-b03f-b8e37d6c5fb2, the contact's ID.
- It looks like a plain text box, so the agent sees an ID they cannot read and could overwrite it with any text.
Note
- Seen from a screenshot. Not checked: whether clicking the field opens a contact picker, whether other lookup fields (Account, Product) behave the same, and what is saved after Run.
- Zoho Desk not checked live.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), ticket #4058, blueprint "test blueprint", Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, 15 Nov 2026.
