# NDC-2832 Tickets | Countdown Mode does not load: 6 of 7 columns show "Couldn't load these tickets"
Summary
Switching a ticket view to Countdown Mode shows the seven columns, but six of them (Overdue, No Due Date, Due in 1 hour, Due today, Due tomorrow, Due later) show "Couldn't load these tickets" and a message "At most 25 filter conditions are allowed." Only the On Hold column loads. Retry does not help, so Countdown Mode cannot be used.
Steps to Reproduce
- Sign in as an admin who belongs to many departments (the owner account here).
- Go to Tickets and open the All Tickets view.
- Open the view-mode menu at the top right and choose Countdown Mode.
- Wait for the columns to load, then click Retry in any column.
Expected Result
- Each column lists the tickets that fall in it, as in Zoho Desk's Countdown Mode (up to seven columns by due time).
- No error message.
Actual Result
- Overdue, No Due Date, Due in 1 hour, Due today, Due tomorrow and Due later all show "Couldn't load these tickets".
- A message appears: "At most 25 filter conditions are allowed."
- Retry fails the same way. Only On Hold shows tickets.
Note for developers
Each column request to the tickets list sends more than 25 filter conditions and is refused with INVALID_FILTERS. Seen as the owner (member of about 40 departments); it reproduced on 9 and 10 Nov.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, 1440×900, owner account, 10 Nov 2026. Compared with Zoho Desk help articles (live Zoho Desk did not load on this network).

Fixed and deployed on staging. Ready for retest.
What I checked: on 16 Nov I opened All Tickets in Countdown Mode as the NDC-Staging owner (about 40 departments) at 1440 px, in a browser on staging-desk.taviportal.com. Every column loaded: Overdue showed 11 tickets, No Due Date 257, and Due in 1 hour and Due today showed "No tickets". All 31 ticket-list requests the board made answered 200, and no column showed "Couldn't load these tickets" or "At most 25 filter conditions".
Why it works now: the board used to send the "not Closed" status exclusion as one condition per status, which pushed each column past the 25-condition limit. Since client-engine commit 3e811660a (NDC-2266, 12 Nov) it is sent as a single condition. That commit is in the running desk-web build.
