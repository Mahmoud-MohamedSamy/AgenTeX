# NDC-2852 SLA | Escalation does not notify the agents chosen in it when they cannot see the ticket
Summary
An SLA escalation can name any agent, including agents from other departments. When the escalation fires, TAVI skips every chosen agent who cannot see the ticket, and only the ticket History shows it. In the test, nobody was notified. In Zoho Desk, the users chosen in the escalation are emailed when the SLA is violated.
Steps to Reproduce
- Go to Setup › SLA & Escalation and create an SLA policy in a department, with a 1-minute first response target.
- In escalation level 1 (at due), choose two agents in "Notify": one from another department and one from this department.
- Create a ticket in the department that matches the policy, owned by another agent, and wait 2 minutes.
- Open the ticket and go to the History tab.
Expected Result
- As in Zoho Desk: an email notification is sent to the users chosen in the escalation when the SLA is violated.
- If TAVI does limit who can be notified, the escalation editor should say so when the agent is chosen, not skip the agent silently later.
Actual Result
- The ticket shows SLA Violated and SLA Escalated.
- History, SLA escalation level 1: "Mahmoud Samy · Skipped: No access to the ticket", "Mahmoud TAVIPortal · Skipped: No access to the ticket", "Skipped: No one to notify".
- No email is sent, and the policy editor gave no warning when these agents were chosen.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, owner account. Zoho Desk behaviour is taken from the Zoho Desk help pages (not checked in a live Zoho Desk, because Zoho Setup pages do not load on our network).
QA AO Dept A, SLA policy "QA AO SL214oya", ticket #2663, 10 Nov 2026 16:01 UTC. Mahmoud TAVIPortal works in Tech only. Ticket data sharing is Private. Zoho source: help.zoho.com "Creating and using SLAs": "An email notification is sent to the selected users when the SLA is violated."
