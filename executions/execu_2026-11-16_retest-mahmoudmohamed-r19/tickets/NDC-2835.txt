# NDC-2835 SLA | A "Before due" escalation longer than the target time can be saved
Summary
An SLA target can respond within 1 hour while its escalation is set to "Before due by 2 hours". That escalation would be due an hour before the ticket even exists. The editor and the server accept it without any message. Levels that are out of order are already refused, so this one check is missing.
Steps to Reproduce
- Go to Setup › SLA & Escalation and click New SLA. Enter a name, tick Ticket Create and click Next.
- Click Add Target. Set Respond Within to 1 Hours.
- Turn on Response Escalation. In Level 1 set When to Escalate to "Before due" and By to 2 Hours.
- Save the target, then save the SLA.
Expected Result
- The target cannot be saved and a message says the Before-due time must be shorter than the target time.
Actual Result
- The target and the SLA save with no message.
- The saved SLA has a 1-hour response target and an escalation 2 hours before it is due.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, 1440×900, owner account, 10 Nov 2026. Compared with Zoho Desk help articles (live Zoho Desk did not load on this network).
