Wave 3 · Automation (workflows, assignment, approvals, reviews, blueprints)

What we tested: every workflow trigger and action, the execution log, assignment rules, approvals, reviews, blueprints and webhooks — 256 automated cases, then re-checks of everything that failed. The 12 tests that expected old behaviour were fixed first, so their results now count.

Interruptions: the first full run was thrown away (our test layouts became unusable after NDC-2650 was fixed — fixed in the tests), and staging sign-in was down from 22:20 to 23:43 (502). Nothing was lost; the runs were repeated.

Also passing: NDC-2420, 2421, 2422, 2411, 2391, 2334, 2323, 2309, 2308, 2298, 2297, 2296, 2540, 2541. The blueprint "Create record" after-action now works (NDC-2294 item 9).
