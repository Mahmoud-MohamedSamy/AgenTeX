C5High

AI | Summary, triage, reply suggestions, draft assist and translate all fail with "Something went wrong"

Cases
W2-67, W2-68, W2-69, W2-70
Where
Ticket page › AI panel (Zia-style tools) and message menu › Translate; API POST /desk/tickets/{id}/ai/*, …/messages/{id}/translate

Steps

  1. Open any ticket, e.g. #3105.
  2. Open the AI panel and press Summarise (also try Triage, Suggest replies, Writing assistant).
  3. On a message, open its menu and press Translate.

Expected

A summary, triage suggestion, reply suggestions, rewritten draft or translation; or a clear message that AI is not set up for the tenant.

Actual

Every action shows "Something went wrong. The AI request failed." The calls return 404 NOT_FOUND. Same on 2 Nov and 12 Nov.

Request and response (from the run, ids shortened)

POST /desk/tickets/{id}/ai/summary  →  404 {"code":"NOT_FOUND","message":"The requested resource was not found."}
POST /desk/tickets/{id}/messages/{id}/translate {target_language:"ar"}  →  404

Zoho Desk

Zoho Desk's Zia gives ticket summary, sentiment, reply suggestions and translation when Zia is enabled; when it is not, the tools are hidden, not broken. Broken on its own whatever Zoho does (related NDC-2004, NDC-2005).
