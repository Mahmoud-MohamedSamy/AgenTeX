What the types do (confirmed)

Moving a ticket into a status applies its type, not its name: a closed-type status such as "QA NDC Pending" sets the closed time, takes the ticket out of the agent's open-ticket count and closes the SLA (resolution met, first response cancelled); an on-hold status pauses the SLA; an open status keeps it running. The admin's open count in Marketing went 35 → 34 → 35 → 35 → 34 → 35 as one ticket moved through QA NDC Pending, On Hold, Escalated, Closed and back to Open.

Findings

ST-2

A status change saved on the record never reaches Desk

Bug · High (proposed)

4 of the 39 moves returned success and the ticket record holds the new status, but the Desk side still says "Open" an hour later. Desk drives the queues, closed time and workload, so "QA NDC Not Approved E-Top-Up Manager" (closed type) has no closed time and still counts as open work. The 4 moves happened around a staging outage (500s at ~09:10 UTC), the same kind of drift as the 17 Nov background-processing problem. Nothing repairs it afterwards.

ST-3

Changing a status type leaves the SLA of existing tickets wrong

Bug · Medium (proposed)

With 3 QA tickets in "QA NDC Distributor": open → closed set their closed time and dropped the workload (4 → 2) at once, but the SLA kept running. Closed → on hold cleared the closed time, and the SLA paused only after 20 s to 2 min, with more time left than before (3,407 → 3,586 s). Back to open: the SLA stayed paused 4 minutes later. The status list was restored exactly afterwards.

ST-4

Ticket APIs return the status but not its type

Gap vs Zoho · Low (proposed)

TAVI's ticket record and Desk ticket return only the status value; the type lives in /desk/ticket-statuses, so every integration or report needs a second call. Zoho returns statusType on every ticket (seen live).
