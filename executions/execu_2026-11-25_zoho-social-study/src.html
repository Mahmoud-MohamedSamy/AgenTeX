Zoho Social: how it works, module by module

A functional walkthrough of Zoho Social (social.zoho.com), written so the team can start building our version. It covers the data model, every module, the business rules and limits, and the integration points with CRM and Desk.

Live trial portal opened 25 Nov ~280 official Zoho help / API pages read Items marked "unconfirmed" need a live check

What Zoho Social is

Zoho Social is a social-media management tool. A company connects its business accounts on networks such as Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn and YouTube, then works on all of them from one screen:

Publish

Write one post, adjust it per network, and publish now, schedule it, queue it, or send it for approval. Calendar, drafts, bulk CSV upload, RSS curation.

Engage

A unified Inbox of comments, DMs, mentions and reviews from every network, with assignment, statuses, saved replies and automation rules.

Listen

Monitor columns that track keywords, hashtags, mentions and other pages, plus a real-time Live Stream of engagement.

Measure

Reports per network (audience, posts, engagement, reach), per-post stats, hashtag reports, custom dashboards, scheduled report emails.

Collaborate

Team roles, approvals, discussions on any post or report, team chat, and an audit log.

Connect to CRM / Desk

Turn a social user into a CRM lead or contact, or turn a post or comment into a Desk ticket, by hand or by rule.

The top navigation in the live app is: Home · Posts · Messages · Inbox · Monitor · Connections · Collaborate · Reports, plus a New Post button (with a dropdown for Customize Content, Create Bulk Message and Bulk Schedule), notifications, team notifications, Settings and the profile menu.

What we saw live

On 25 Nov we started a Zoho Social trial on our Zoho test account (an Enterprise trial, 15 days). These are the facts from the real product:

  1. Sign-up creates a portal at once. The URL is social.zoho.com/social/<portal-name>/<brand-id>/Home.do. The portal name comes from the account name, and a numeric brand ID is assigned before any brand is set up.
  2. A mandatory onboarding form comes first. It asks for the main objective (Publishing/Scheduling, Reporting/Analytics, Social Monitoring), company size, and a mandatory phone number. The app does not load until it is submitted.
  3. Nothing works until one channel is connected. Every tab (Posts, Inbox, Monitor, Reports and the rest) shows the same "Get started by setting up a Brand" wall. The wall shows 15 network tabs, each with its own "Connect <network>" button that opens that network's sign-in. The only other option is Manage → Delete Portal.
We did not connect a real social account, so we could not open the inner screens (composer, Inbox, Reports). Their details come from Zoho's official help center and API docs. Exploring them live needs a test Facebook Page or X account that the team owns. See Open questions.

Data model

Four objects hold everything else. Getting these right first matters most, because every screen is filtered by the current brand.

ObjectRules
PortalCreated at sign-up and named after the company. The first user becomes Portal Owner and Portal Admin. One Zoho login can belong to several portals (switch under My Portals). Portal settings: Portal Info (company name, portal name, owner, Delete Portal), My Settings (time zone), Customization (Agency plans), Email Authentication (DKIM). Deleting a portal erases all brands, posts, drafts, columns and reports for good, and is only allowed on the Free plan.
BrandA group of channels for one business or business unit. The first brand is created automatically when the first channel is connected. Only one channel of each type per brand (one Facebook Page, one X profile, one LinkedIn Page, one LinkedIn Profile, …). A second Facebook Page needs a second brand. Fields: name, image, description, time zone. Deleting a brand deletes its scheduled posts; posts already on the networks stay. A brand switcher sits top-left.
ChannelOne connected network account, holding an OAuth token. Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn tokens expire after 60 days, and the admin must then Reconnect (email alert plus a Brand Health alert). Disconnect removes the channel's drafts, scheduled and pending posts, Inbox history, Monitor columns, automation rules and dashboard cards. The first Facebook connection imports the last 30 days of data.
MemberA user in a brand with a role, a set of channels they may use (checkbox per channel), and an optional approver flag. Removing a member keeps their scheduled posts (they still publish), but their Monitor columns are deleted.

Home dashboard

  • Brand Health card: last 30 days per channel: Total followers, New followers, No. of posts, Reach, Engagement (likes + comments + shares). Green means up and red means down. It also holds "Add more channels" icons and "needs reconnecting" alerts. X and TikTok have no Reach; GBP has no follower or post counts.
  • Recent Posts: latest published posts with their audience response.
  • Live Stream (right panel): a real-time feed of likes, comments, shares and mentions (Facebook, X, Instagram, Bluesky). Each item allows Reply, Like, Retweet, DM, Follow, +Add to CRM, Convert to Ticket and Discuss. It cannot be filtered.
  • The layout is fixed and cannot be customized.

Five ways to send a post

ActionBehaviour
Publish NowGoes out at once.
Custom ScheduleDate and time (one time for all selected channels). Shows the % of the audience likely online. Optional Repeat: weekly (one weekday) or monthly (day of month, within 6 months; day 31 = "last day" or "whenever available"), until an end date. No daily repeat.
Add to Queue (CustomQ)Takes the next free slot of the brand's queue. Admins define slots per weekday (max 25 per day; slots can be cloned to other days). One post per slot. Changing slots does not move posts already queued.
SmartQSuggests best times for the next 7 days, sorted by engagement or time, from ≥7 days of history (Facebook, Instagram, X only). Premium+.
Save as Draft / Send for ApprovalA draft is also kept if the window is closed before scheduling. Approval: see the next section.

Bulk Scheduler

  • Upload CSV, XLS or XLSX, max 350 posts per file. A sample file is offered.
  • Columns: Schedule Time, Post Content, Link, Media, GMB Button Name, GMB Link, plus an optional channels column (in CSV the channel list goes in quotes: "Facebook, X, Instagram"). Pinterest uses IMAGE URL 1…n.
  • The date format is chosen in the upload window and must match exactly. Each post must be ≥1 hour after upload. Media are public URLs (.jpg/.jpeg), max 10 per post.
  • Flow: Upload › date format › shortener on/off › channels › Pinterest board › confirm column order › approval toggle › Proceed to Review (each row is "completed" or "with errors"; error rows can be fixed, moved to drafts or deleted) › Preview › Schedule.

Pause / resume (brand-wide)

Settings › Publishing Preferences › Pause Posts (admins; asks for a team message). Banner: "All scheduled posts for this Brand are currently paused." Users can still schedule. On resume, posts whose time has passed go to Unpublished › Unattempted for rescheduling.

RSS feeds

Posts › Content Library › RSS Feeds › +New Feed (site search, feed URL, or OPML upload). 10 feeds on Professional, 20 on Premium+. Each feed has default channels plus a prefix and suffix text. Each article can be composed into a post, marked read, moved to drafts, hidden or discussed. Auto-publishing from RSS is not documented.

Approvals

Yet to Review⇄Under Review→ApprovedorRejected (reason required)·Time Elapsed (auto)
  • Approvers are set in Brand Settings › Publishing Preferences › Add Approver. An approver must have publishing rights on all channels of the brand. Portal and Brand Admins are approvers by default. Clients can be approvers.
  • Single level: any one approver decides. All approvers get an email. Only single-brand posts can go for approval.
  • Approve dropdown: Approve and Publish Now, Approve and Add to Queue; otherwise the sender's schedule is kept. Approved and Rejected are final.
  • Time Elapsed is set automatically when the scheduled time passes unapproved; the post can still be approved and rescheduled.
  • Editing an approved post sends it back for a new approval. A rejected post can be edited and resubmitted.
  • Bulk Approve All / Reject All, max 200. A Post Activity panel logs submissions, comments and status changes.

Media, links, AI

Social Library

A shared brand store of images, GIFs and videos (images ≤36 MB, video ≤5 GB). Upload from desktop, cloud picker, stock library or Canva.

Cloud Picker

Zoho WorkDrive, Google Drive, Box, OneDrive, Dropbox, Evernote, Zoho Notebook. Single posts cannot take images by URL; only the Bulk Scheduler can.

Link shortener

Built-in zurl (default), Bit.ly (Professional+), or a custom domain (A record or CNAME). Short links can have a title, expiry, fallback URL and UTM tags.

UTM tracking

Auto-adds UTM tags to every link when turned on: source (auto = network name, or custom), medium, campaign (≤20 chars), content, term, custom; max 6 parameters.

Zia AI

Compose (social post, hashtags, IG captions, X threads, rephrase; tone, language, prompt) and Inbox reply drafts. Monthly credits: Free 5, Standard/Pro 40, Premium+ 80. Customers can bring their own OpenAI key instead.

Optimization

Optional auto-conversion of video and images to each network's specs (re-encode, scale, bitrate). Image fit: black background or skew.

Inbox (unified interactions)

One list of every interaction, which is a comment, DM, review, mention, reply, question or ad ("dark") comment. Networks: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, GBP, YouTube, TikTok, WhatsApp, Telegram, Threads, Bluesky. Premium+.

Threading rules

SituationResult
New message, previous interaction closed less than 24 h agoThe closed interaction reopens
New message after 24 h, interaction Open or Under ReviewAdded to the same interaction; its time moves to the newest message
New message after 24 h, interaction ClosedA new interaction is created; the old one can no longer be replied to
Your own commentsNever create interactions
"Reply and Close" setting onReplying closes the interaction. For messages this counts only after 24 h; for other types it applies at once

Interaction panel and fields

  • Status: Open, Under Review, Closed (one page also names "On Hold": unconfirmed). Priority: Low, Medium, High. Assignee (called "DRI" for LinkedIn). Sentiment: Positive, Neutral, Negative.
  • Tabs: Reply box (with Zia), Activity (status changes, internal comments, @mentions), User Info (editable email, contact, website, location, description), History (all past interactions with that user).
  • Actions: Copy link, Follow, Archive, Mute user, Mark as spam, Delete; bulk mark read, spam or archive.
  • Views: All, Open, Archived, Spam, plus custom views (name, channels, assignee, type, priority, status, period; can be shared and pinned). Filters: channel, type, assignee, priority, status, date, unread.
  • Saved replies: max 280 chars, in groups. Not for WhatsApp, which uses its approved templates.
  • Private reply to a public comment (Facebook, Instagram): one per comment, within 7 days.

Automation rules

Settings › Inbox Preferences › Automation › Create New Rule: name, description, Match ANY / ALL, conditions, actions.

Conditions (13)

  • Channel · Interaction type · Content (exact, contains, starts/ends with…)
  • Content type (image, video, text) · Sentiment · Verified user
  • Followers count (>, <, =, between) · Creation time (days/hours)
  • Sender · First-time interaction · Language
  • CRM contact (known/unknown) · CRM lead

Actions (10)

  • Set priority · Change status · Set assignee
  • Round robin (all, selected or role-based members)
  • Send a reply · Send a private reply
  • Move to archive · Mark as spam · Close
  • Send alert (email or in-app, optional delay)

Inbox preferences: Reply and Close, muted users, auto-spam keywords, auto-archive closed items after 90 or 120 days, purge archived items after 15 or 30 days, Facebook and Instagram ad accounts for ad comments.

WhatsApp and Telegram

  • WhatsApp: messages are paid with credits (1 conversation = a 24 h thread). Outside the 24 h window only an approved template can be sent. Template fields: category (utility or marketing), name, group, header (text or image), body, footer, buttons (call-to-action or quick reply). WhatsApp approves templates, usually within an hour. Bulk messaging: lists of up to 200 contacts (manual or CSV/XLS), sent as separate 1:1 messages, now or scheduled.
  • Telegram: a bot token; users are reached by username; quick replies.

Messages tab

A simpler DM-only view, separate from Inbox and available from Professional: Facebook Pages, X, Instagram, LinkedIn Pages and Bluesky. Attachments: Facebook and Instagram take 1 audio (WAV/MP4) or 1 video (MOV/MP4) up to 25 MB, and voice notes can be recorded up to 2 min; X takes video up to 512 MB. Network rules: Facebook cannot start a conversation (reply only); X can start a DM only if the user allows DMs from anyone; Instagram needs "Allow access to messages" turned on; LinkedIn shows the last 60 days only.

Connections

People who interacted with the brand are created automatically. Views: All, Recently Engaged, Most Engaged, X Followers and Following, Restricted (muted or blocked), CRM Leads, CRM Contacts. Profile panel: contact details, social profiles, interaction history, CRM Info (with a Ping chat to the CRM owner), Desk Info, and Add a Note with files.

Collaborate & notifications

  • Discuss exists on drafts, scheduled, published and failed posts, Monitor items, connections and report cards. Visibility: Everyone, Team Members (default) or Custom Share. Up to 10 images per discussion. @tag members, and clients too. The Collaborate feed filters: Discussions, Drafts, Posts, Scheduled, Unpublished, Connections, Reports.
  • Team chat and audio/video calls from any member's avatar (Premium+).
  • Social notifications (bell): Facebook visitor posts, comments, messages, likes; X mentions, messages, retweets, likes, replies; Instagram comments. Per type, an admin picks which members get them.
  • Email notifications: scheduled post published, scheduled post failed, channel disconnected, @mention in a discussion, approval requests and status changes.
  • Team notifications: user joined, discussion started or updated, item shared, posts paused or resumed.

CRM / Desk integration

This is the part closest to our existing TAVI CRM and Desk, and probably the main reason to build Social inside our suite.

Zoho CRM

  • Brands sync both ways between Social and CRM's Social tab; the same Facebook Page and X profile must be connected in both.
  • +Add to CRM from Live Stream, Monitor, Messages, Connections: Associate to Existing (search a lead or contact) or Add New (lead or contact, choose CRM layout, fill fields).
  • Lead generation rules per channel: Social Action (like, comment, mention, DM…) + keyword criteria → add as Lead or Contact, with an on/off toggle.
  • Connection profile shows CRM info, owner (Ping chat) and CRM notes. Inbox rules can test "is a CRM contact / lead".
  • Only Facebook Page and X (network API limits).

Zoho Desk

  • Convert to Ticket from Monitor, Live Stream and Posts: pick a Department › Convert. A ticket ID is shown and links to Desk.
  • Automatic ticket creation (Settings › Ticket Creation) per Facebook and X: post and message types, append interval (replies inside it join the same ticket), keyword → department routing.
  • Connection profile shows Desk info: tickets, owner, average response and resolution time, happiness rating.
  • Reports › Desk Tickets lists all tickets created from Social.
  • Only Facebook and X.

Zoho Social Lite comes free with paid Zoho CRM or Desk: 1 brand, 2 members, 6 core channels, CRM/Desk integration, mobile apps. Other integrations: Canva, cloud storage, Bit.ly, Zoho Flow (draft, schedule and publish actions; no triggers), a browser extension (zShare), iOS and Android apps, and Zoho MCP (31 AI-assistant tools).

Public API v2 (useful as a reference design)

Base https://social.zoho.com/social/v2, Zoho OAuth 2.0, headers portal_id and brand_id. Zoho support must enable it for each portal. Times are Unix ms. There are no webhooks.

Post payload: post.messages[], each with channels[] (network, brand_id, mentions, geo_location, first_comment, poll, has_dm, call_to_action, board_id), message, link_preview, medias[] (path, type, size, width, height, alt_text). Schedule: schedule_time, type = schedule | repeat, repetition {weekly|monthly, start, end, day}. Network keys: facebookpage, twitter, linkedinprofile, linkedinpage, instagram, googlemybusiness, youtube, pinterest, tiktok, mastodon, threadsbyig, bluesky, arattai. Errors: INVALID_PARAM, INSUFFICIENT_PERMISSION, CHANNEL_NOT_ALLOWED, PLAN_NOT_SUPPORTED, BRAND_NOT_ALLOWED, API_NOT_ALLOWED.

Suggested build order

This is a recommendation from the study, not a Zoho fact. Each phase is usable on its own, and the later ones reuse the data model from phase 1.

  1. Foundation: portal, brand, channel and member model; roles with the 7 permissions and per-channel access; OAuth connect, reconnect and disconnect for Facebook Page, Instagram, X and LinkedIn first; token-expiry job and alerts; audit log.
  2. Publishing core: composer with per-network customization and validation against network limits; media upload and library; publish now, schedule, repeat, drafts; a publishing worker with retry, Failed and Unattempted states; Posts lists and calendar.
  3. Team workflow: approvals (5 states, single level), queue slots (CustomQ), pause and resume, bulk CSV scheduler, notifications (email + in-app), discussions.
  4. Engagement: webhook or polling ingestion per network; Inbox with the threading rules, statuses, assignment, saved replies, custom views, automation rules; Messages; Connections.
  5. CRM / Desk link: Add to CRM (associate or create), lead-generation rules, Convert to Ticket, auto ticket rules with append interval, CRM and Desk info on profiles. Reuse TAVI CRM and Desk APIs.
  6. Insights: metrics collection jobs per network; Brand Health; per-network reports, post stats, export, scheduled report emails; then Monitor columns and Live Stream.
  7. Later: SmartQ (needs history), hashtag reports, custom dashboards, more networks (YouTube, GBP, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky, Pinterest, WhatsApp, Telegram), AI compose and reply, agency client portal.
