Desk Skills: TAVI vs Zoho Desk

Every Skills function was run on both products at runtime, with QA data created in each. Rows marked "Not compared" were not run on one side.

Correction to the 11 Nov "Skills out of layout" draft

The draft said Zoho has no Skills field in the ticket layout. That is wrong. Zoho lists Skills (Lookup, entitySkills) as a standard, removable field in Ticket Information, both in the layout editor and in Fields List, and shows it in the ticket's properties. Zoho only leaves it off the Add Ticket form.

So TAVI keeping Skills as a layout field matches Zoho. The real problem is finding 3: the create form shows the field, and its options are not limited to active skills of the ticket's department. The draft should be rewritten around finding 3 and not filed as it stands.

TAVI extras (not bugs)

  • Skill names up to 100 characters (Zoho 50). HTML characters are accepted and shown as plain text; Zoho refuses them.
  • A skill may have no criteria; it is then only added by hand or by a workflow. Zoho requires criteria.
  • "Where used" lists assignment rules, workflow rules and actions, other skills' criteria, agents and open tickets. Zoho lists related rules only. Approval processes are still missing from TAVI's list (NDC-2805).
  • Recalculate skills for many tickets from the Skills page, with progress. It runs on every open unowned ticket in all departments, not only the department shown on the page.
