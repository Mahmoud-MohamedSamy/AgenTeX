# NDC-2994 Lead Conversion | Convert page shows no message when the server refuses the conversion [Done]
Summary
When the server refuses a lead conversion (422), the convert page shows no message. The user does not know why nothing happened.
Environment
- NDC-Staging (staging-crm.taviportal.com), Chromium, 12 November 2026, admin account.
- A test lead converted with a new deal (Transaction).
Steps to reproduce
- Open a lead and click Convert.
- Choose to create a new deal and set the stage to Closed Lost. Leave amount, closing date and reason empty.
- Click Convert.
Expected result
The page shows the server's message (amount, closing date and reason are required), as Zoho CRM does on its convert page.
Actual result
- The request returns 422 (amount, closing date and reason required).
- The page shows nothing and stays as it was.
Notes
- Found while testing Layout Rules case G15.
- Evidence: crm-G15-convert.png (attached).
