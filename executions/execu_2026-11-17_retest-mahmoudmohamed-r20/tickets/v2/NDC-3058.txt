--- 2026-11-17T17:20:12.142145+02:00 by 82cf7a79-0707-4f47-97ca-f8926587b6db
 Fixed and live on tavi (client-engine #852, metadata-engine #238, workflow #143, deployed 17 Nov 2026). Moving to Ready For Test. 

 Following the QA lead's decision, a rule can now create a tag, as in Zoho Desk. In Zoho a rule belongs to one department, and so does the tag it creates. Here:

 
 A rule limited to one department offers only that department's tags (plus tags for all departments), and typing a new name offers "Create". The tag is created in that department. You need permission to manage tags there, as on Tickets › Tags.

 A rule over several departments (or all) names each tag with its department ("Refund · Billing") and says to limit the rule to one department to create a tag.

 A tag a rule adds to a ticket must belong to the ticket's department, or to all departments. Another department's tag is not added.

 Every action a rule carries out at save, Add Tags included, now appears in Execution History.

 
 Checked on NDC Staging:

 
 Browser: a rule limited to QA AO Dept A offered only QA WF tag B and C (A was already chosen). Typing a new name offered Create, and the tag was created in QA AO Dept A and added to the rule. I deleted the test tag and the rule afterwards.

 API: a rule over all departments adding Customer Support's "Accepted" and QA AO Dept A's "QA WF tag A" to a QA AO Dept A ticket. Only "QA WF tag A" was added, and Execution History shows Add Tags, Succeeded.

 
 To know: on a rule over several departments, Add Tags shows Succeeded even when one of its tags belongs to another department than the ticket's and was not added.
