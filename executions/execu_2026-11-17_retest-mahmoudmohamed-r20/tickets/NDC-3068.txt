# NDC-3068 Ticket History | Closed Time shows a raw timestamp and Resolution Time shows a bare number
Summary
After a ticket is closed, History shows "Closed Time was updated" with a raw ISO value in UTC and "Resolution Time was updated" with a bare number and no unit.
Steps to Reproduce
- Close a ticket (here: through a blueprint transition into Closed).
- Open the ticket › History.
Expected Result
- Closed Time in the user's date/time format and time zone (e.g. 16/11/2026 11:48 AM); Resolution Time with a unit (e.g. 11 minutes).
Actual Result
- "Closed Time · New 2026-11-16T09:48:59.7799208+00:00" (#4177) and "2026-11-16T09:09:14.975698+00:00" (#4166).
- "Resolution Time · New 11" / "New 3" with no unit, while Key Information shows "Less than a minute".
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge (headless) on Windows 10, 16 Nov 2026, owner account ndc-staging-owner.
