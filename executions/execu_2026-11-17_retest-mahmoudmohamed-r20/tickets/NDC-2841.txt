# NDC-2841 SLA | A contract in one department can use a support plan (and its SLA) from another department
Summary
Support plans and SLA policies belong to a department, and a contract belongs to a department. Desk accepts a contract in one department with a support plan from another department, so the other department’s SLA is applied to this customer. In Zoho Desk a contract is created inside its department and the Support Plan field only lists that department’s plans.
Steps to Reproduce
- In Setup › Support Plans, select department B and create a support plan with an SLA policy of department B.
- Create a contract in department A (with an account, start and end date) and choose the department B plan as its support plan.
- Open the contract.
- Go to Setup › Support Plans and select department B.
Expected Result
- Only department A’s support plans can be chosen for a department A contract; a plan from another department is refused.
Actual Result
- The contract is saved. Its Department is QA AO Dept A, and Support Plan Info shows the QA AO Dept B plan and its Dept B SLA.
- Support Plans for QA AO Dept B lists that plan with Contracts = 1.
- Attaching a Dept B plan to an existing Dept A contract was also accepted on 9 Nov.
- Note: the contract was created through the API; whether the New Contract form also offers other departments’ plans was not checked.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, owner account, 10 Nov 2026 12:10 UTC. Zoho Desk (techsolutionsegypt) checked live, read-only: Add Contract under the current department; plans loaded with departmentId of the current department.
