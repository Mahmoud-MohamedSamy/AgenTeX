# NDC-3064 Tickets | A ticket has no Edit button for all its properties, and no way to add, change or remove its contact
Summary
On the ticket page, the Ticket Properties panel has no Edit button to change all of a ticket's fields in one form. The Contact Info card also has no option to add a contact when there is none, or to change or remove the linked contact.
Steps to reproduce
- Open ticket #4173 "QA test" (Tech).
- Look at the top of the Ticket Properties panel.
- Look at the Contact Info card, which shows "No contact on this ticket."
- Open a ticket that has a contact, for example #4164 (contact Mahmoud Mohamed), and look at its Contact Info card.
Expected result
- An Edit button on Ticket Properties opens all the ticket's fields in one form, saved together.
- In Contact Info, an agent can add a contact when the ticket has none, change the linked contact to another one, and remove the linked contact.
Actual result
- No Edit button. Fields can only be changed one at a time, inline.
- Contact Info shows only "No contact on this ticket." and has no button.
- On a ticket with a contact, the card shows the name, email and phone with no change or remove option.
- The only way a contact gets onto a ticket is through the reply "To" address ("The first address is the ticket's contact").
Additional notes
- Zoho Desk has a ticket Edit form and lets agents change a ticket's contact from the ticket (not checked live this time).
- The ticket in the screenshot is in a blueprint, but the blueprint only locks Status, so the blueprint is not why these options are missing.
- Annotated evidence attached: box 1 (FAIL) the Ticket Properties header has no Edit button; box 2 (FAIL) the Contact Info card has no add, change or remove option; box 3 (INFO) fields that can be edited inline one at a time.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Edge, 16 Nov 2026.
