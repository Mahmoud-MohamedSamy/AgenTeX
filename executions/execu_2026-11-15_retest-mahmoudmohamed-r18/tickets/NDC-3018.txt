# NDC-3018 Validation Rules | Screen readers are not told why a save was refused
Summary
When a validation rule refuses a save, the message appears under the field but is not linked to it or announced, so screen-reader users get no feedback.
Environment
- Staging, tenant NDC-Staging, 12–13 Nov 2026
- Owner account; Leads and Tickets record forms
- Test data used the prefix "QA VR1112" and was deleted after the check.
Steps to reproduce
- Create a rule that refuses a value.
- Open a new record, type the refused value and click Save.
- Inspect the field and the message in the page.
Expected result
The field has aria-invalid="true", aria-describedby points to the message, the message is announced (role="alert" or a live region), and focus goes to the refused field.
Actual result
- No aria-invalid and no aria-describedby on the input (CRM and Desk).
- No role="alert" or aria-live region.
- CRM moves focus to the refused field; Desk moves focus to Subject instead.
Notes
- Case M4.
- Evidence: crm-M4-refused.png, desk-M4-refused.png (attached).
