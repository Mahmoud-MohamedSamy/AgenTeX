# NDC-3005 Web Forms | A form set to Arabic keeps English field labels and left-to-right layout
Summary
Arabic web form keeps English labels and left-to-right layout. Found in the Desk go-live retest, Wave 4 (W4-41), NDC-Staging, 12 Nov 2026. Where: Setup › Web Forms › form language Arabic.
Steps
- Set a web form's language to Arabic.
- Open the form.
Expected
Arabic field labels and right-to-left layout.
Actual
Only the buttons change (إرسال, إعادة تعيين). Field labels stay English (Last Name, Email, Subject, Description) and the page direction stays left-to-right.
Zoho Desk
Zoho Desk supports field translations for 20 languages (help page: Adding field translations).
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com, NDC-Staging (Wonder Travel), owner and a customer mailbox, Edge, 12 Nov 2026.

Annotated evidence: W4-D4-arabic-form-english-ltr.png.
