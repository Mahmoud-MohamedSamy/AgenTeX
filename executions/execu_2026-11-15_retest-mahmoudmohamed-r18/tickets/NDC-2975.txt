# NDC-2975 Layout Rules | Inline edit gives no way to fill a field the rule makes mandatory
Summary
Inline edit gives no way to fill a field the rule makes mandatory. TAVI shows "QA LR1112 order is required." and offers no way to fill it.
Environment
- NDC-Staging (staging-crm.taviportal.com), Chromium, 12 November 2026, admin account.
- Leads layout "QA LR1112 Layout" with test fields type (Refund, Question, Partner, Other), tier, filter (pick lists), order, note, reason (text), vip (checkbox), radio, date and email2. Test data was deleted after the run.
Steps to reproduce
- Open a record.
- Inline-edit type to Refund and confirm.
Expected result
Zoho opens a "Review Details" pop-up asking for the mandatory field, with Cancel and Save.
Actual result
- TAVI shows "QA LR1112 order is required." and offers no way to fill it.
- The change cannot be saved from the record page.
Notes
- Test cases G4 in the Layout Rules test sheet (https://claude.ai/artifact/PUdj9axtAjCR8UQpegu5qC).
- Evidence: crm-G4-inline-after.png, zoho-G4-picked.png (attached).
