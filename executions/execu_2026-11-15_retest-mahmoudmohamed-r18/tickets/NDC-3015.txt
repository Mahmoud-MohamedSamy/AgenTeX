# NDC-3015 Validation Rules | Deleting a layout leaves its rules in the list, Active, pointing to a missing layout
Summary
A layout that has active validation rules can be deleted. Its rules stay in the list and stay Active, pointing to a layout that no longer exists.
Environment
- Staging, tenant NDC-Staging, 12–13 Nov 2026
- Owner account; Leads and Tickets
- Test data used the prefix "QA VR1112" and was deleted after the check.
Steps to reproduce
- Create a layout and an active rule for it.
- Delete the layout.
- Open the Validation Rules list and the rule.
Expected result
The layout delete warns about the rules, and the rules are removed or switched off and flagged.
Actual result
- The layout is deleted (204) with no warning.
- The rule stays listed and Active; GET shows conditionError "Layout … is not a layout of this module".
Notes
- Case J6.
