# NDC-3044 Ticket History | A customer reply from the Help Center is shown as "Email received"
Summary
When a customer replies to their ticket from the Help Center, the ticket History tab records it as "Email received", as if the reply came by email.
Steps to Reproduce
- Create a ticket for a contact who can sign in to the Help Center.
- As the contact, open the ticket in the Help Center and post a reply.
- As an agent, open the ticket › History.
Expected Result
- The entry says the reply came from the Help Center, e.g. "Reply received from the Help Center".
Actual Result
- The entry reads "Email received from <contact name>" (seen on QA ticket #3463, 13 Nov 2026, 14:35 Cairo).
Note
Not compared with Zoho (not checked live). Found during Blueprint case K3: the reply also moved the ticket correctly through the blueprint, only the History label is wrong.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, owner account, 13 Nov 2026.
