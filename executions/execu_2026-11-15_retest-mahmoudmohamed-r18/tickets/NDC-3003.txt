# NDC-3003 Email Templates | A department reply-acknowledgement template cannot be switched off or deleted
Summary
A department reply-acknowledgement template cannot be switched off or deleted. Found in the Desk go-live retest, Wave 4 (W4-28 (cleanup)), NDC-Staging, 12 Nov 2026. Where: Setup › Email Templates.
Steps
- Create a reply-acknowledgement template for one department (Marketing) and Activate it.
- Open its Actions menu, then the built-in Reply acknowledgement's Actions menu.
- Try to Delete your template.
Expected
A way to go back to the built-in template for that department (Deactivate, or Activate on the built-in), then Delete works.
Actual
Your template's menu shows Activate (as if not active). The built-in's Activate is greyed out: "This template is already the one being sent." There is no Deactivate. Delete is refused: "This template is the one being sent. Activate another template, or the built-in one, before…". The template is stuck.
Zoho Desk
Not checked live in Zoho (it would need test data in the Zoho account, which is read only). This is TAVI failing its own feature, so no Zoho comparison is needed to call it a bug.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com, NDC-Staging (Wonder Travel), owner and a customer mailbox, Edge, 12 Nov 2026.
