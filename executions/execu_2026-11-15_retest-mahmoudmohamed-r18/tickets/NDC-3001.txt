# NDC-3001 Knowledge Base | Text typed in the article translation editor comes out reversed
Summary
KB translation editor reverses typed text. Found in the Desk go-live retest, Wave 4 (W4-69), NDC-Staging, 12 Nov 2026. Where: Knowledge Base › article › Translations tab › Body.
Steps
- Open a published article and go to the Translations tab.
- Pick a language (French or Arabic) and click into Body.
- Type "abc def 123".
Expected
The text appears as typed.
Actual
Every key lands at the start of the box, so the text is saved as "321 fed cba" (Arabic too). Pasting works. A translation cannot be typed by hand.
Zoho Desk
Not checked live in Zoho (it would need test data in the Zoho account, which is read only). This is TAVI failing its own feature, so no Zoho comparison is needed to call it a bug.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com, NDC-Staging (Wonder Travel), owner and a customer mailbox, Edge, 12 Nov 2026.
