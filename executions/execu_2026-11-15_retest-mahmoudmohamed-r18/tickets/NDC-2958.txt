# NDC-2958 Workflow Rules | ${recordId} and ${moduleId} come out empty in a Field Update
Summary
The editor help says ${recordId} and ${moduleId} are always available, but they are replaced with nothing when the rule runs.
Steps to Reproduce
- Create a rule On Create with a Field Update: Description = R[${recordId}] M[${moduleId}] S[${subject}].
- Create a matching ticket.
- Open the ticket.
Expected Result
- ${recordId} and ${moduleId} are replaced with the ticket's ID and the module.
- As in Zoho Desk, where ${Cases.Request Id} gives the ticket number, no listed placeholder comes out empty.
Actual Result
- Ticket #3160 shows "R[] M[] S[QA AO WF EV5 ticket]": the two placeholders are empty; ${subject} works.
Zoho Desk
Help "Creating and managing email templates": ${Cases.Request Id} is replaced with the ticket's ID/number.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, owner account, 11–12 Nov 2026. Compared live with Zoho Desk (test account, nothing left behind) and Zoho Desk help articles.
