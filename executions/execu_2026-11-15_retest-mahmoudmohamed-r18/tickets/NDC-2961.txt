# NDC-2961 Workflow Rules | Rules list shows internal field names in the Trigger column
Summary
The Trigger column shows internal field names and "1 days". Zoho Desk shows field labels.
Steps to Reproduce
- Go to Settings › Automation › Workflow Rules.
- Look at the Trigger column of rules that run on a field change or relative to a date.
Expected Result
- Field labels, as in Zoho Desk: "Field Changed: QA NDC Related Branch", "Field Changed: Ticket Owner", "1 day before Due Date".
Actual Result
- "Field Changed: qa_ndc_related_branch", "Field Changed: owner", "Field Changed: status", "Date-Field Relative: due_date (1 days before)". Same in Arabic.
Zoho Desk
Rules use field labels everywhere; internal names are never shown.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, owner account, 11–12 Nov 2026. Compared live with Zoho Desk (test account, nothing left behind) and Zoho Desk help articles.
