# NDC-2948 Workflow Rules | A criteria pattern that leaves out a condition is accepted, and that condition is ignored
Summary
When the criteria pattern does not use every condition number, the rule is saved and the missing condition is silently ignored. Zoho Desk refuses such a pattern.
Steps to Reproduce
- Go to Settings › Automation › Workflow Rules and click New Rule (Tickets).
- Add 3 conditions.
- Click Edit Pattern, type 1 and 2, and click Apply.
- Click Save & Activate.
Expected Result
- The pattern is refused with a message like Zoho's: "Numbers in this pattern do not match with the numbers used in the Criteria Editor".
- The rule cannot be saved until every condition number is used.
Actual Result
- The rule saves.
- Condition 3 stays on the screen but is not part of the criteria, so the rule fires even when condition 3 is false.
- Other pattern errors are handled well: "Pattern ends unexpectedly.", "Missing closing parenthesis.", "Pattern refers to condition 4, which doesn't exist."
Zoho Desk
A pattern "1" with 2 conditions shows "Numbers in this pattern do not match with the numbers used in the Criteria Editor" and is not applied.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), Microsoft Edge, Windows 10, owner account, 11–12 Nov 2026. Compared live with Zoho Desk (test account, nothing left behind) and Zoho Desk help articles.
