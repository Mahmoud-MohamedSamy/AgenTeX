# NDC-3006 Knowledge Base | The image Gallery stays empty after uploading or inserting images
Summary
KB Gallery is always empty. Found in the Desk go-live retest, Wave 4 (W4-66), NDC-Staging, 12 Nov 2026. Where: Knowledge Base › Gallery.
Steps
- Upload an image in Gallery, and insert images into an article.
- Open Gallery again.
Expected
The uploaded and inserted images are listed for reuse (the page says images inserted into articles land there too).
Actual
Gallery never lists anything: its list call /files?purpose=kb_article_image returns [] although 3 images were uploaded or inserted.
Zoho Desk
Not checked live in Zoho (it would need test data in the Zoho account, which is read only). This is TAVI failing its own feature, so no Zoho comparison is needed to call it a bug.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com, NDC-Staging (Wonder Travel), owner and a customer mailbox, Edge, 12 Nov 2026.
