# NDC-2976 Layout Rules | Deal stage bar refuses a stage change with no way to fill the mandatory field
Summary
Deal stage bar refuses a stage change with no way to fill the mandatory field. TAVI refuses with "approval note is required" and opens no form.
Environment
- NDC-Staging (staging-crm.taviportal.com), Chromium, 12 November 2026, admin account.
- Leads layout "QA LR1112 Layout" with test fields type (Refund, Question, Partner, Other), tier, filter (pick lists), order, note, reason (text), vip (checkbox), radio, date and email2. Test data was deleted after the run.
Steps to reproduce
- Rule on a Deals layout: Stage = Negotiation and discount > 20 makes the approval note mandatory.
- On the deal page click the Negotiation stage.
Expected result
Zoho asks for the mandatory field in a pop-up before moving the stage.
Actual result
- TAVI refuses with "approval note is required" and opens no form.
- The deal stays in Qualification.
- Same pattern as B-02.
