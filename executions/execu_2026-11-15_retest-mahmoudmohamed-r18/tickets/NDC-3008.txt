# NDC-3008 Email | Blocked addresses accept a wildcard (*@example.com) although the page says one address at a time
Summary
Blocked addresses accept a wildcard (*@example.com). Found in the Desk go-live retest, Wave 4 (W4-13), NDC-Staging, 12 Nov 2026. Where: Setup › Channels › Email › Blocked addresses.
Steps
- Block "*@example.com".
Expected
Either refused (the page says "Desk blocks one address at a time, not a whole domain") or a real domain block.
Actual
"*@example.com" is accepted and listed as blocked, with no hint of what it does.
Zoho Desk
Zoho Desk's Allowlist and Blocklist blocks emails and domains ("tickets from blocked emails and domains will be restricted and marked as spam"). So domain blocking is expected; TAVI should support it properly or refuse the wildcard.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com, NDC-Staging (Wonder Travel), owner and a customer mailbox, Edge, 12 Nov 2026.

Annotated evidence: W4-D2-wildcard-blocked-vs-zoho.png.
