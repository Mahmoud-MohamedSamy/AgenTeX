BH-1 · Business Hours | The saved business hours can't be saved again (400), even unchanged

Summary. The tenant's business hours are stored in a state the app's own check refuses. The record says "24×7" but also holds working hours 09:00–17:00. Saving it again, even without any change, answers 400, so nobody can save business hours until the stored record is fixed.

Steps to reproduce.

  1. Open Settings › Company › Business Hours (or GET the business-hours setting).
  2. Without changing anything, click Save (or send the same body back with PUT).

Expected result.

  • An unchanged save succeeds. A 24×7 setting should not store working hours at all, so the stored record and the check agree.

Actual result.

  • The stored record: preset 24_7, timing mode "same", hours 09:00–17:00, Africa/Cairo.
  • Saving it unchanged answers 400 business_hours.invalid_timing_mode.
  • The check itself works: switching Sunday off is refused with "24_7 requires all 7 days".
  • Likely cause: on 26 Oct someone switched the tenant to 09:00–17:00 and later back to 24×7; switching back kept the hours.

Environment. NDC-Staging · ndc-staging-owner (Owner) · 27 Oct 2026, tenant-wide window (step 1b). Nothing was changed. Annotated image attached.

US-08 · Users | The owner can choose a lower profile for themselves with no warning

Summary. On the owner's own user record, picking a lower profile such as "User" enables the Assign button without any warning. If the owner confirms, they would lose owner rights, and the Owner profile can't be given to any other account (409 OWNER_PROFILE_LOCKED), so nobody could give it back.

Steps to reproduce.

  1. Sign in as the owner. Open Settings › Users › your own user.
  2. In Profile, choose "User (rank 100)".
  3. Look at the Assign button and any message. (Do not click Assign.)

Expected result.

  • The owner can't remove their own owner rights, or gets a clear warning that explains the result and asks to confirm. Zoho doesn't let the super admin change their own profile.

Actual result.

  • Assign is enabled and no warning is shown.
  • Giving the Owner profile to another account is refused (409 OWNER_PROFILE_LOCKED), so the change could not be undone.
  • Assign was not clicked in the test.

Environment. NDC-Staging · ndc-staging-owner (Owner) · 27 Oct 2026, Wave 1 leftover US-08. Nothing was changed. Annotated image attached.

PE-06 · Permissions | Without access, the Permissions page shows the raw text "Missing required permission 'iam.profile.manage'"

Summary. A user who may not manage profiles can still open Settings › Permissions by its address. The page shows the raw server text "Missing required permission 'iam.profile.manage'." instead of a normal "no access" page. There is also no view-only permission for profiles, so a read-only view can't be given to anyone.

Steps to reproduce.

  1. Sign in as a user whose profile doesn't include managing profiles (e.g. test user 1, built-in User profile).
  2. Open https://staging-crm.taviportal.com/settings/security/permissions.

Expected result.

  • The page is hidden from the menu and opening it by address shows the app's normal "You don't have access" page. Optionally, a view-only permission lets someone see profiles without changing them (Zoho shows profiles read-only to users who can view but not edit).

Actual result.

  • The page opens and shows "Missing required permission 'iam.profile.manage'."
  • /iam/profiles answers 403. The permission catalogue has only iam.profile.manage, no view-only key.

Environment. NDC-Staging · test user 1 · 27 Oct 2026, Wave 1 leftover PE-06. Annotated image attached.

SEAT (optional) · Users | Inviting with no free seat shows the raw key "iam.subscription.seat_limit_reached"

Summary. When the tenant has no free user seat, inviting a user is refused, but the message is the raw key "iam.subscription.seat_limit_reached" instead of a sentence telling the admin to free or buy a seat.

Steps to reproduce.

  1. With all seats used, open Settings › Users › Invite and invite any email.

Expected result.

  • A clear message, e.g. "All seats are in use. Remove a user or add seats in Billing to invite someone."

Actual result.

  • The invite answers 409 SUBSCRIPTION_SEAT_LIMIT_REACHED and the screen shows "iam.subscription.seat_limit_reached".

Environment. NDC-Staging · ndc-staging-owner (Owner) · 27 Oct 2026, Wave 1 leftover US-13. No invite was created. Annotated image attached.
