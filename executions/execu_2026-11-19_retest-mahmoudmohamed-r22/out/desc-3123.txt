Summary
Disabling a department with "Transfer to" now moves its tickets to the chosen department. The dialog does not say this: it says the department's tickets are kept, and the transfer line mentions only agents and channels.
Steps to Reproduce
- Setup › Departments › ⋮ on a department that has tickets › disable.
- Read the dialog.
- Choose a department in Transfer to and click Transfer and Disable.
- Open one of the department's tickets.
Expected Result
The dialog says the tickets (and how many) will move to the chosen department.
Actual Result
Step 2: "The department is not deleted. Its tickets, history and configuration are kept, and enabling it again brings the department back." and "It currently has 1 agent(s) and 1 channel(s). Choose an active department to transfer them to." Step 4: the ticket is now in the chosen department.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com, NDC-Staging, Edge + API, owner account (ndc-staging-owner); found 17 Nov 2026 during the findings retest, N1 checked again 18 Nov. Test data deleted after.
