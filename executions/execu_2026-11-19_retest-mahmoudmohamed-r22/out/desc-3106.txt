Summary
A currency added and made active in Settings › Company Settings › Currencies can't be used on a record. The record Currency field (Deals and other modules) is a separate fixed pick list, and a record's currency must be in both lists. The Currencies section itself says "Records can be in any active currency".
Environment
- TAVI CRM: staging-crm.taviportal.com, tenant NDC-Staging, account ndc-staging-owner
- Zoho CRM (reference): test org, checked live in Edge
- Date: 17 Nov 2026
Steps to reproduce
- Go to Settings › Company Settings › Currencies and add Euro (rate 50, Active).
- Create a Deal (Transactions) with Currency = EUR and Amount 1000, then save.
- Create another Deal with Currency = USD (a value in the Currency list that was not added in Settings), then save.
Expected result
The record Currency field lists the active currencies from Settings (home currency plus EUR), and the deal saves in EUR. This is how Zoho CRM works: Euro appears in the Deal Currency field straight after it is added.
Actual result
- EUR is refused: "Currency: 'EUR' is not one of the allowed values (EGP, USD, AED, JOD, KWD, OMR, SAR)."
- USD is refused: "Currency USD is not one of the organization's currencies."
- A record saves only when the code is in both the fixed pick list and Settings. Then the exchange rate and home value are filled correctly.
