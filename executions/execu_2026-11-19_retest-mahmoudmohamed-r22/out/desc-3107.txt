Summary
On Create Transactions (Deals, Standard layout) there is no Currency or Exchange Rate field, so the user can't choose the record's currency. The Amount box shows "$" although the home currency is EGP.
Environment
- TAVI CRM: staging-crm.taviportal.com, tenant NDC-Staging, account ndc-staging-owner
- Zoho CRM (reference): test org, checked live in Edge
- Date: 17 Nov 2026
Steps to reproduce
- Make sure the home currency is EGP (Settings › Company Settings › Currencies).
- Open Transactions › New (Standard layout).
- Look at the Amount box and look for a Currency field.
Expected result
Amount shows the home currency (EGP) until another currency is chosen, and the form has Currency and a read-only Exchange Rate. In Zoho CRM both fields are added to the module when a second currency is added, and they appear on Create Deal.
Actual result
- Amount and Expected Revenue show "$".
- No Currency or Exchange Rate field on the form. They appear only on the saved record, under Additional Information.
