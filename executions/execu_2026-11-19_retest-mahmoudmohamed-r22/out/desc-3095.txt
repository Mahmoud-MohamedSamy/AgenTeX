Summary
The ticket list gives no way to move a ticket to another department. Rows do not show the department, and moving is only possible from ⋮ › Move on the ticket page. Found in the Desk go-live ticket access run (departments, teams, sharing, moving), NDC-Staging, 17 Nov 2026.
Steps
- Sign in as an agent or admin who belongs to more than one department.
- Open Tickets › All Tickets (any list view).
- Look for the ticket's department on a row and try to move the ticket from the list.
Expected
As in Zoho: each row shows its department, and clicking it opens a "Move Department" picker that moves the ticket from the list.
Actual
Rows show subject, number, time, status and owner only. There is no department label and no move action in the list; the agent has to open each ticket and use ⋮ › Move.
Zoho Desk
Checked live 17 Nov: All Departments list, Classic view — every row shows its department (e.g. "Tech ▾"); clicking it opens "MOVE DEPARTMENT" with the other departments. (Zoho's mass-selection bar has no Move either, so bulk Move is not part of this report.)
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com, NDC-Staging (Wonder Travel), Edge, 17 Nov 2026; accounts: owner, Desk Administrator (B), Desk Agent (C, Customer Support), Test User 1 (Desk Agent / Light Agent for the test). Zoho Desk checked live the same day in the test org TechEgySolutions (admin, test agents 1 and 2); test data deleted and settings restored after.
