Summary
When a ticket is cloned from a department where the current user is not an agent, the Clone form shows Ticket Owner "Unassigned". Saving without changing it creates the clone owned by the current user (the create request sends no owner). The form does not show what will be saved.
Steps to Reproduce
- As the owner account, open a ticket of a department the owner is not an agent of (e.g. QA RT19 SLA Dept), owned by another agent.
- ⋮ › Clone; look at Ticket Owner.
- Save without changing it.
- Look at the new ticket's owner.
Expected Result
The saved owner matches the form. In Zoho Desk (checked live 17 Nov) Clone keeps the source ticket's owner.
Actual Result
Step 2: "Unassigned". Step 4: #5081 owned by ndc-staging-owner (the user who cloned), 17 Nov 17:57 Cairo.
Environment
staging-desk.taviportal.com, NDC-Staging, Edge + API, owner account (ndc-staging-owner); found 17 Nov 2026 during the findings retest, N1 checked again 18 Nov. Test data deleted after.
