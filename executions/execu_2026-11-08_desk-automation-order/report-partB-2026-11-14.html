Differs OR-13 · Round robin cannot see the SLA on a new ticket · filed NDC-3046

A round-robin rule whose condition is "SLA name equals the policy" did not assign the new ticket, although the SLA applied to it (history: ticket.created › sla.applied(create), no assignment). After a later edit, the same rule assigned A1 at once. So on create, round robin does not see the SLA result (it either runs first or checks an earlier copy of the ticket). Effect: a round-robin rule that depends on the SLA (name, violated, due) never matches on create. Filed 14 Nov as NDC-3046 (Medium, Bug, Sprint 16) with annotated evidence.
