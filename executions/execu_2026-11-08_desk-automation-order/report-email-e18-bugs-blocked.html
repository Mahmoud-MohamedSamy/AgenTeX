BugMediumEM-08

Email setup says "Mail is arriving" even when customer mail does not reach Desk

Summary

The Email Overview page shows a green "Mail is arriving" status and "4 of 4 done", but it has no way of knowing whether mail actually arrives. On 9 Nov a customer reply never reached Desk while the page showed exactly this status, so an admin is told everything is fine when it is not.

Steps to reproduce
  1. Go to Setup › Channels › Email › Overview.
  2. Look at the status card at the top.
  3. From a customer mailbox, reply to an email that an agent sent from a Desk ticket.
  4. Wait 40 minutes and open the ticket in Desk.
  5. Go back to the Email Overview page.
Expected result
  • If customer mail is not arriving, the page warns about it.
  • The page shows when the last email was received, so the admin can see whether mail really arrives.
Actual result
  • The page shows a green "Mail is arriving. Customers can write in, and replies go out. Nothing is waiting on you." and "4 of 4 done".
  • There is no time of the last email received anywhere on the page.
  • The same card says "The mail provider did not answer when we checked taviportal.com. This says nothing about whether mail is arriving."
  • The customer reply (sent 9 Nov 3:30 PM Cairo) never appeared on the ticket or as a new ticket, while the status stayed green.
Environment

TAVI Desk staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), owner account, Edge; Overview seen 9 Nov and 10 Nov 2026 09:31 UTC; receiving address customer-support@help-staging.anyware.software; customer mailbox mmohamed@anyware.software.

Note

The other session drafted this as plane-draft-mail.html (not posted). The receiving cases EM-01/02/04/05 are still blocked; this bug stands on its own because the page shows the same status whether mail arrives or not.

GapLowE18-03

Round robin backlog skips an old ticket without saying why

Summary

The 30-day rule works: a backlog ticket with no customer reply in the last 30 days is not assigned. But nothing tells the agent or admin why. The ticket just stays unassigned in the backlog with no entry in its History, while newer tickets are assigned within seconds.

Steps to reproduce
  1. In a department with a round robin rule, turn on "Assign tickets to offline agents" and set the department threshold to 1, so new tickets wait in the backlog.
  2. Create a new ticket that matches the rule; it waits unassigned.
  3. Reopen an old ticket (created more than 30 days ago, no customer reply), move it to the same department and clear its owner; it waits unassigned.
  4. Turn the department threshold off and turn on "Assign backlogs".
  5. After a few minutes, open both tickets and look at the owner and the History tab.
Expected result
  • The new ticket is assigned from the backlog.
  • The old ticket is left out (30-day rule), and its History, or the backlog view, says why, for example "Not assigned: no customer reply in the last 30 days".
Actual result
  • The new ticket (#2453) was assigned to Test User 1 13 seconds after release; its History says "Taken from the backlog".
  • The old ticket (#1) was still unassigned 32 minutes later.
  • Its History shows only the reopen, move and owner change; nothing says it was skipped or why.
  • Same result on 9 Nov (twice) and 10 Nov.
Environment

TAVI Desk staging-desk.taviportal.com (NDC-Staging), owner account; QA AS6 Dept B, rule "QA E18 manual"; 10 Nov 2026 10:02–10:36 UTC.

Note

The rule itself passes. Drafted as a gap (missing explanation), not a defect. Zoho Desk was not checked live for a skip reason.
