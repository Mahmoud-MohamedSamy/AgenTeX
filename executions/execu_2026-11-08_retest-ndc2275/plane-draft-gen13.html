GEN-13 import: follow-up, 9 Nov 2026

The 8 Nov retest left GEN-13 "to decide". It is now decided: rules don't run on import, and rows with no owner stay with the person importing (as in the 6 Nov fix note). Only the wording on the Assign step needs a change.

Compared with Zoho Desk (test account, 9 Nov, temporary round robin rule on Ticket Create, 2-row import with no owner column):

  • Import wizard → no rule, owner or automation option on any step. TAVI has no rule picker either. [SAME]
  • Round robin on imported tickets → Zoho did not apply it (#104, #105: History only "New ticket created through import"). TAVI doesn't either, box ticked or not (#1681–#1684). [SAME]
  • Same rule on a ticket made by hand → Zoho assigned it (#103, "Ticket was assigned through a Round Robin Rule"); TAVI assigns it too (#1660). [SAME]
  • Owner of rows with no owner → Zoho leaves them Unassigned; TAVI gives them to the person importing. Kept as designed (QA decision). [DECISION]

Please change

  • Import → Assign, help text under "Trigger automation and process management": "Owner assignment from the chosen rule runs regardless". No rule can be chosen on import, so this should say what happens instead, for example: "Rows without an owner are assigned to you."

The requirement line "import (rules on) is assigned by the same matching rule" no longer applies. NF-3 (threshold exceeded by one under simultaneous creates) is still open, so the state stays Bug.
