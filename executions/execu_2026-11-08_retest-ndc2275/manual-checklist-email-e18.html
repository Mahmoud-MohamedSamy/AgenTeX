Manual checklist: Email channel and 30-day rule

0 / 0 steps · NDC-Staging · prepared 9 Nov 2026

How to use this

Tick each step as you do it. For every case, fill in the send time, the result after 15 minutes and anything you noticed. Entries are kept in this browser only (they survive a reload). When done, press Export results and send the file, plus your screenshots.

Why two customer mailboxes: the receiving address is on help-staging.anyware.software, a sub-domain of anyware.software. Mail sent from an @anyware.software mailbox may be delivered inside the company's own mail system and never reach TAVI. A Gmail/Outlook address tells us whether receiving fails for everyone or only internally.

1 · Email channel (confirms the bug)

Write down the send time for each case. Check TAVI after 2, 5 and 15 minutes.

EM-01 · New email from an outside address creates a ticket

RECEIVE

Expected: a new ticket in Customer Support, channel Email, contact = the Gmail address, your text as the first incoming message.

Shows whether receiving works at all.

EM-02 · New email from the company domain creates a ticket

RECEIVE

Expected: a new ticket, as in EM-01.

EM-01 passes but EM-02 fails → mail between anyware.software addresses (routing), not TAVI. Both fail → TAVI isn't receiving.

EM-03 · Agent reply reaches the customer

SEND

Expected: the reply shows "Sent" on the ticket and arrives in Gmail from Customer Support, subject "Re: …".

Passed on 9 Nov (to mmohamed@).

EM-04 · Customer reply attaches to the same ticket (the bug)

RECEIVE

Expected: within a few minutes the ticket shows your text as an incoming customer message; conversation count +1; no new ticket.

9 Nov (from mmohamed@): nothing arrived after 42 minutes, neither on the ticket nor as a new ticket.

EM-05 · Same as EM-04 from the company mailbox

RECEIVE

Expected: the reply appears on the ticket as an incoming message.

With EM-04, shows whether the failure depends on the sender's domain.

EM-06 · Second receiving address

RECEIVE

Expected: a new ticket in the Tech department.

Shows whether only Customer Support or every address is broken.

EM-07 · Nothing is held back silently

ADMIN

Expected: any email that did not become a ticket or reply is listed with a reason.

9 Nov: lists empty; Quarantine and Spam returned "An unexpected error occurred" at 3:55 PM.

EM-08 · The Email page tells the truth

ADMIN

Expected: if mail did not arrive, the page does not say "Mail is arriving … Nothing is waiting on you" or "4 of 4 done"; it warns, or shows when mail was last received.

9 Nov: "Mail is arriving", "4 of 4 done", plus "The mail provider did not answer when we checked taviportal.com".

EM-09 · No bounce reached the customer

MAILBOX

A bounce means mail never reached TAVI's mail server (routing/DNS). No bounce plus nothing in TAVI means TAVI received it and dropped it.

2 · 30-day rule (E-18)

Unassigned and backlog tickets are assigned by round robin only if the customer replied within the last 30 days.

BACKLOG

30-DAY

9 Nov: seen twice exactly like this. #1's History does not say why it was skipped.

E18-04 · A customer reply makes the old ticket assignable

30-DAY

Only after EM-04 or EM-05 passes (incoming replies work).

Expected: #1 is now assigned to Test User 1, because the customer replied today. With E18-03 this proves the rule is based on the last customer reply, not the ticket's age.
