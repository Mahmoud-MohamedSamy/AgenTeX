Description (as it will appear)

Summary

The ticket page has no Skills tab. In Zoho Desk the side rail of a ticket has a Skills icon that opens a Skills panel: it lists the skills on the ticket (or "No skills yet") and has + Associate Skills, with the text "Associate skills with this ticket to automatically assign it to agents who possess those skills". In TAVI Desk the side rail has no Skills icon; skills show only as the plain "Skills" field under Case Information.

Steps

  1. Sign in to Desk staging and open any ticket (for example #1767 or #955).
  2. Look at the icons in the side rail next to Ticket Properties.
  3. Scroll Ticket Properties to Case Information.

Expected (as in Zoho Desk)

  • A Skills icon in the ticket side rail.
  • The Skills panel lists the skills on the ticket, or "No skills yet".
  • "+ Associate Skills" adds skills to this ticket from the panel, so skill-based assignment can use them.

Actual

  • The side rail has no Skills icon (ticket #955 shows only Ticket Properties and AI assist).
  • Skills appear only as the "Skills" field under Case Information ("-" when empty).
  • No Associate Skills action on the ticket page.

Environment

  • staging-desk.taviportal.com, 9 Nov 2026, Edge.
  • Seen on two tenants: ndc-staging-owner (ticket #1767) and Anyware (ticket #955).
  • Zoho Desk reference: ticket #100, 9 Nov 2026.
